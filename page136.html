Kane became the first English winner of the Ballon d’Or since 2001

Harry Kane thanked his family, players and coaches, past and present, as he held back tears after winning the 2026 Ballon d’Or at London Palladium.

The England captain and Bayern Munich striker beat Lamine Yamal and Kylian Mbappe to world football’s most prestigious individual prize at the London Palladium, rewarded for a staggering season in which he scored 73 goals for club and country.

Kane, who spent 19 years at Tottenham before moving to Bayern in 2023, became the first Englishman since Michael Owen in 2001 to lift the accolade.

The 33-year-old won the German league and cup double for Bayern while also guiding his club to the Champions League semi-finals, where they lost to winners PSG.

He then led his country to the last four of the World Cup, where they suffered a devastating loss to Argentina. England nevertheless achieved their best World Cup finish since 1966, coming home with bronze after victory over France in the third-place play-off.

Here’s his full acceptance speech at the London Palladium:

“Wow, wow, wow. I'm almost speechless. Lucky I have this [his speech] here. First of all, thank you again to France Football, to the Ballon d'Or for putting on this amazing event. Being here in London as well has made it extra special and looked like a good omen for me tonight, so thank you very much.

“Thank you to everyone who voted for me. I can imagine it was really tough this year. Some of the incredible players I was up against, some of my team-mates and world-class players in the room tonight, so thank you to everyone who voted for me. Congratulations to all the winners, all the nominees tonight - an impressive showing of what determination and hard work can really achieve, so well done.

“My Bayern Munich team-mates, I've got a few here tonight. Michael [Olise], Upa [Dayot Upamecano], Lucho [Luis Diaz], Lenny [Lennart Karl]. Without you guys and the rest of the team, it wouldn't be possible. The way you guys push me, the way we compete, me against Upa in training every week, it just makes me a better player, makes me want to improve, so thank you very much.

“To Vinnie [Vincent Kompany], I know he's at home or I think still in the hospital so wishing you very well. Hope this has gave you a bit of energy. To Vinnie and all the staff, the way we train, the way you push us, it's a credit to yourself. Since I've been with you, I've improved as a player, no doubt, improved as a person, as a leader, and that's thanks to your leadership.

“To Jan [Jan-Christian Dreesen, Bayern CEO], to Herbert [Hainer, Bayern president], to all the Bayern Munich chairmans and presidents, I know some of you couldn't be here tonight, but it's an honour to play for Bayern Munich. To represent you is a real privilege, so I'm glad to be bringing this trophy back to Bayern Munich for sure.

“To all my England teammates, we had a special year. It wasn't the perfect year, but we had a lot of good moments. We built a great bond together, a lot of togetherness. The way we trained, the way we played, the togetherness in the hotel in the World Cup. It’s truly an honour to captain you, lead you. It makes me extremely proud so this one is for all of us, for sure.

“To Thomas Tuchel and the staff at England again. The leadership, the way you drive us, the way you push standards is something to be admired and it's an honour to represent you and represent the England badge the way you do. Freiburg will hope to lick their Bundesliga wounds with a victory against visitors Wolfsburg on Wednesday at Europa-Park Stadion in the DFB-Pokal's second round.

The hosts suffered a 3-1 defeat against Bayern Munich on Sunday, and after a difficult period of results, they will be keen to bounce back against their 2. Bundesliga opponents.

Few teams would have managed to take any points from Bayern, but the fact Freiburg allowed centre-back Dayot Upamecano to open the scoring after he charged out from his own penalty area unmarked will be immensely disappointing.

That result was their third consecutive loss in all competitions, with the club losing 3-0 against Monaco in the Conference League on October 22, as well as 2-1 against Bayer Leverkusen in the league four days prior.

Julian Schuster's side are third in the table with 13 points, and while the head coach could argue that a four-point gap to the top two is not insurmountable, only three points separate them from eighth-placed Elversberg.

Breisgau-Brasilianer managed to beat Dusseldorf 5-1 in the first round of the DFB-Pokal on August 23, and while they were eliminated in the second round in 2023-24, they have reached at least the semi-finals of the competition three times since 2021–22.

Freiburg had a perfect record at Europa-Park Stadion this season prior to their clash with Bayern, winning all five of their contests at the ground before the weekend.

Wolfsburg were in the top flight in 2025-26, only to suffer relegation to the 2. Bundesliga after finishing in the 16th-placed relegation playoff spot and losing against Paderborn in their two-legged tie.

The Wolves are currently fourth in the second tier with 16 points from nine league fixtures, and they are just two points from the third-placed promotion playoff spot.

Tobias Strobl's side are winless in their last four meetings with their hosts, with his team losing three times, though he was not in charge for any of those games.

Wolfsburg are unbeaten in their four most recent fixtures, emerging as victors twice while scoring 13 goals and conceding seven times.

The visitors have played six away matches this term, though they have drawn two and lost one of their past three outings on the road, and they only came out on top against hosts Altglienicke in the DFB-Pokal in August following a penalty shootout.

Freiburg will believe they have a strong chance of winning the DFB-Pokal, so it would not be surprising if starters like Igor Matanovic and Jan-Niklas Beste started in attack.

However, with Yannik Engelhardt a doubt due to a hip issue, it would be surprising if Schuster risked him in midfield, meaning Patrick Osterhage could partner Maximilian Eggestein.

Goalkeeper Florian Muller may come into the XI, and the shot-stopper could be protected by centre-backs Max Rosenfelder and Philipp Lienhart.

Wolfsburg midfielder Mattias Svanberg has been ruled out until the middle of November, so perhaps Yannick Gerhardt and Vinicius Souza will be stationed together in a double pivot.

If the Wolves are to win, they will need forwards Alexander Bernhardsson, Muhammed Mehmet Damar, Alessio Besio and Robert Glatzel to be at their best.

#3908. Here’s Another Mass Effect 5 Leak To Break Your Heart

📅 Oct 26, 2026  |  Category: Other  |  Read Source →

If last week's mysterious Mass Effect: Universe trailer leak wasn't painful enough for you in the wake of BioWare's sudden closure, we've gotten yet another taste of The Game That Could Have Been, this time courtesy of award-winning video game composer Austin Wintory. 

Known for his beautiful and haunting soundtracks for games like Flow, Journey, ABZÛ, Aliens: Fireteam Elite, Sword of The Sea, John Wick Hex, and Hades II, Wintory was also evidently working on the Mass Effect: Universe soundtrack. From the sounds of things, EA and the now-defunct BioWare were considering having Wintory produce the game's OST. And thanks to Wintory, we at least know what the game might have sounded like.

"Mass Effect was my ultimate white whale, my all-time dream project," Wintory wrote in a post on Bluesky. "For the last couple years I'd been in discussion with BioWare & EA to take on the project, and as part of that, I wrote close to 20 minutes of music to explore where the franchise can go next. May as well share it!"

Wintory shared the music alongside an image that reads, "Mass Effect: Universe, the music that might have been."

"So here you go," Wintory added in a follow-up post linking to a YouTube upload of the test-run tracks. "Thank you again to my friends at both BioWare and EA. To even dream in your sandbox was a privilege."

The video features 20 minutes of Wintory's experimental score, featuring seven unique tracks and one remix. Text displayed within the video itself also reveals more about the nature of Wintory's talks with EA and BioWare, along with his inspiration for the soundtrack.

"In 2007, when the credits rolled on my first playthrough of Mass Effect, I remember calling a friend and saying, 'I can't believe I already know this will be among my favorite all-time games.' Usually, it takes the passage of time before we can be so sure something truly belongs to the ages, but in this case, the confidence was instantaneous," Wintory wrote. "This trilogy is my most-cherished gaming experience of my lifetime, and as a professional composer, represented my most sought-after 'White Whale.' For me, there is no greater dream project to be part of."

According to Wintory, nothing had been finalized yet, but both EA and BioWare seemed eager to get him on board. So Wintory, in all his love for the franchise, cooked up a little surprise for the Mass Effect: Universe team.

"For the past several years, I'd been in dialogue with people both at BioWare and EA to potentially jump into Mass Effect 5," Wintory explained. "As part of those conversations, I decided to surprise the team by writing/recording a giant love letter to the franchise, with my idea of where it can go next. When I wrote this (in 2025), I knew nothing of what they were planning, so this was a bit narratively speculative. The concept was a direct homage to Jack Wall and Sam Hulick's incredible work on the first game, while leaning even harder on true analog synths, and a more complex integration of the orchestra (almost akin to the hybrid nature of The Matrix)."

While it seems that Mass Effect: Universe will never see the light of day, at least we have the beautiful soundtrack-that-could-have been, for what looked to be an equally beautiful game.

#2029. What Harry Kane must do to build on deserved Ballon d'Or triumph

📅 Oct 26, 2026  |  Category: Sports  |  Read Source →

The stage was set for Harry Kane and he stepped onto it for his crowning moment.

For so long a duopoly of Lionel Messi and Cristiano Ronaldo, the Ballon d'Or has been opened up to the masses in recent years. The outstanding candidates for this particular edition also came with obvious caveats.

Kylian Mbappe shone individually but did not win a major trophy for club or country. Lamine Yamal had an understated World Cup after he came back from injury. Rodri only played half a season. Khvicha Kvaratskhelia was outstanding for Paris Saint-Germain but did not get the opportunity to play at the World Cup.

All that considered, Kane found himself as favourite. There was also the added draw for him of the Ballon d'Or ceremony being staged in London for the first time ever.

The Bayern Munich striker does not have the allure and excitement surrounding him of Mbappe or Yamal. Nor does he have the PR machine behind him of the Spanish clubs. With no World Cup or Champions League trophy to push his case either, he was relying on the sheer weight of his numbers.

Those numbers, to his credit, were remarkable. Kane scored 73 goals in 65 matches for Bayern and England last season.

That proved enough. It has not always been the case, but on this occasion the best player in the world last season was rightfully awarded the Ballon d'Or.

For a striker as remarkably complete as he is, Kane has repeatedly suffered frustration when glory has been within touching distance.

Across defeats in the final of two European Championships and a Champions League, Kane did not score. He managed a grand total of two shots across those three matches.

The criticism put to Kane for much of his career has been that he has not delivered on the biggest stage, that his goal tallies had not been translated into trophy hauls.

He won the Bundesliga and the German Cup last season but that point still very much remains. Had he dragged Bayern to the Champions League or England to the World Cup, the Ballon d'Or voting would surely have been a landslide.

Instead, he was quiet in the World Cup quarter-final against Norway and had no impact at all in the semi-final defeat to Argentina. That has happened too often. The one last step for him to take is delivering in those kind of matches.

Kane scored six goals at the tournament but it was Jude Bellingham who was the primary source of decisive moments in attack.

When the lights are shining brightest, Kane is yet to have that one standout match. A final or even a knockout match against elite opposition when he had stood up, scored the goals, stamped his authority on the occasion and carried his side over the line.

Goals in the Bundesliga are a given for Kane. So too the title, even if Bayern contrived not to win it in his first season at the club. Those are not how he should be judged.

Kane was a worthy winner of the Ballon d'Or but that nagging doubt over the trophy cabinet remains. It is Champions League glory or international silverware that would now complete his career.

Bayern are only the fourth favourites for the Champions League this season, behind Barcelona, Paris Saint-Germain and Arsenal.

There is no sign of Kane slowing down but the next World Cup is a long way away. His last big chance of leading the Three Lions to an international trophy, then, could be in 2028, when England have a Euros on home soil to target.

Adding his name to the list of Ballon d'Or winners ensures Kane will go down as a great of the game.

Individual prizes, though, have never been the issue for a goalscorer of his calibre. The final years of his career must be about adding meaningful, major team honours to go with it.

#3978. Complete Moon Sweepers walkthrough

📅 Oct 26, 2026  |  Category: Business  |  Read Source →

Moon Sweepers is all about cleaning Earth’s satellite, and all you have to do is clean every square inch of the Moon. However, there are multiple ways to accomplish this goal, and this can make things confusing.

The basic premise of Moon Sweepers is simple. You begin your adventure on the Moon with the sole objective of completing different contracts. After all, cleaning the Moon isn’t an act of philanthropy. The Lunar Dust you get can be sold for profit, and you’ll then get to get new tools and upgrades.

Here’s a quick rundown of what we will be doing.

As you might have understood, there is no one fixed way of cleaning the moon. First, let’s check out how to clean the surface of the moon before delving deeper into the more advanced concepts.

Your first task is to establish a base. Once done, begin cleaning areas that are closer to your base. At this stage, you do not have vehicles to move around, and you also do not have plenty of advanced tools. You are likely going to only have the toothbrush, and this is more than fine.

Clean as many sectors as possible with the toothbrush without spending any more money. While you can get a better tool, like the nail brush or the hand brush, it’s better to save them once you have cleared at least 15 sectors.

Each cleaning tool in the game has its own cleaning capacity and carry capacity. While it might sound very lucrative, do not purchase all the tools. Instead, prioritize the ones that will give you a distinct advantage.

For example, go to the Household Broom directly after using the Toothbrush. The Household Broom is a massive upgrade when it comes to collecting lunar dust. Once done, it’s better to avoid the Wide Broom or the Extra-Wide Broom. Instead, we will focus on the upgrades to improve our collection rate.

Now, let’s look at all the upgrades. There are five different categories available for you to pick from.

If you play long enough, you can eventually get all the upgrades, but when you are starting out, you will need to prioritize the ones that are useful. For example, Market Contracts is the first economy-based upgrade that you should always unlock.

It improves the valuation of every contract you fulfill and allows you to get more money. The more money you have, the more upgrades and tools you can get, making your life a lot easier.

Once you have passed the beginning stages, you will eventually craft your garage. The garage is an important addition that comes at a cost of $200. Once you have your garage, you will be able to purchase different vehicles. Vehicles begin with a Push Sweeper priced at $300 and go all the way up to a Mega Vacuum Rover that costs a steep $6,000.

The objective of every vehicle is the same: they allow you to clean the moon more efficiently. Due to their higher speed, you will be able to travel to different areas of the moon more quickly. With patch 1.0.3, we can also add plenty of upgrades to our vehicles, making them much more efficient.

For myself, I did not have vehicles set up immediately. Once I had enough dollars, I began with a couple of Push Sweepers that do the basic job pretty well. Acquiring the likes of a Mega Vacuum Rover might sound lucrative, but you should always save them for the end game.

At the beginning, you will be selling lunar dust because there are no other options. Eventually, you will look to sell Moon Sand and produce Moonstone. These are much more valuable resources that can be produced from Lunar Dust, and they will make you rich in quick time.

To produce Moon Sand, you’ll need to purchase the Pressure Chamber. This will convert 5 Kg of Lunar Dust into Moon Sand every 12 seconds. It works automatically, so you don’t have to do anything yourself.

Once you have Moon Sand, you’ll need to get a Stone Press. This will consume 1 Kg of Lunar Dust and 1 Kg of Moon Sand to produce 2 kg of Moonstone Block every six seconds.

For this guide, I haven’t touched automation for two reasons. First, I am working on a separate guide for it that will explore all forms of automation available in the game. Second, automation is a strict endgame activity. Once you reach that stage, you won’t need this walkthrough since you have aced every basic and advanced concept.
