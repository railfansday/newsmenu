#3292. LeBron Log No. 2: Sixers firsts, Philly kicks and a night at center

📅 Oct 27, 2026  |  Category: Business  |  Read Source →

LeBron Log No. 2: Sixers firsts, Philly kicks and a night at center originally appeared on NBC Sports Philadelphia

LeBron James has played more NBA seasons than some of his teammates have been alive. Philadelphia still managed to give him a week full of firsts.

Over six days, James made his first regular-season start as a Sixer, scored his first points, played his first regular-season home game, recorded his first double-double with the team, sat out for the first time, started at center and experienced his first loss.

Not every first deserves a plaque. Together, they made for a busy opening chapter.

Welcome to the second edition of the LeBron Log.

James' first assist as a Sixer also produced Jaylen Brown's first points with the team.

With 8:58 remaining in the first quarter against the Knicks, James found Brown cutting to the basket for a layup. Joel Embiid returned the favor later in the quarter, finding James inside for his first Philadelphia points.

Brown assisted James' first Sixers dunk early in the third quarter. James finished the alley-oop to tie the game at 58 and helped Philadelphia turn a four-point halftime deficit into a seven-point lead entering the fourth.

James finished his debut with 14 points, nine rebounds, seven assists, one steal and one block. More importantly, the Sixers left Madison Square Garden with a 106-100 win after watching the Knicks raise their championship banner.

Two nights later, James' first shot in his first regular-season home game became his first three-pointer as a Sixer. VJ Edgecombe found him on the left wing for a 27-footer that gave Philadelphia an early lead over Cleveland.

The evening became far more memorable from there.

James recorded his first Sixers double-double with 17 points, 10 rebounds, five assists and three steals. Philadelphia erased a 29-point deficit for the largest comeback win of James' career and the franchise's largest in the play-by-play era, which dates to 1996-97.

He also received his first complete introduction to the Philadelphia crowd. The Sixers were booed into halftime and cheered off the floor after their 114-106 win.

"We got the boos," James said. "That was amazing, and we deserved it."

His daughter Zhuri celebrated her 12th birthday courtside, while his mother was also in attendance.

James began his Sixers career at Madison Square Garden wearing a purple-blue Nike LeBron 24 player-exclusive with iridescent branding.

The inspiration behind the colorway has not been revealed. House of Heat reported that the shoe could be tied to one of the five themes behind the LeBron 24 line: wonder, family, fear, luck or discovery.

For his home debut, James went with something unmistakably Philadelphia.

He wore a red Nike LeBron 24 on his left foot and a blue one on his right. The mismatched "Philly" edition featured a textured knit upper, metallic lace hardware and James' crown logo on the tongue.

Only 76 pairs were released, a nod to his new team. They were made available through James' Welcome to Philly pop-up, which ran from Oct. 20 through Oct. 22 at 901 Market East.

James did not have to choose between the Sixers' two primary colors. He wore one of each.

James sat out Saturday's win over Milwaukee as the Sixers managed the lower-back pain and sciatica that has followed him since last season.

He returned Sunday to find a very different assignment waiting for him.

Embiid was unavailable for the second half of the back-to-back. Jabari Walker, Dominick Barlow and Tacko Fall were also sidelined, leaving the Sixers short on frontcourt options against Detroit.

For only the 15th time in his career, James started at center. It was his first start at the position since the 2021-22 season.

James won the opening tip against former Sixer Paul Reed and scored 18 points on 8-for-15 shooting. He added five assists and two rebounds in Philadelphia's 106-93 loss.

Do not expect the experiment to become permanent.

"Well, s—, we got an MVP over there," James said. "I'm not here to have a sustainable role at the five."

The Sixers were outrebounded 54-37 and surrendered 24 second-chance points. When James was asked how the team could improve on the glass, he borrowed an answer from former Lakers teammate Taurean Prince.

Prince happened to be sitting on Detroit's bench Sunday. His blunt explanation of rebounding after Baylor's 2016 NCAA Tournament loss to Yale remains one of college basketball's great press-conference clips.

"In the great words of Taurean Prince, who I saw tonight on the bench, a good friend of mine, the ball goes up into the air, and then you go grab the ball," James said.

Adem Bona did much of the traditional work at the position. He grabbed 13 rebounds, including five on the offensive end, in a season-high 25 minutes.

"I thought he was amazing tonight," James said. "His energy, his effort."

James can play center when the Sixers need him there. He also made it clear that he did not come to Philadelphia to take Embiid's job.

James and Edgecombe connected for the former's first home basket. Their next notable connection did not work out as well.

During the third quarter against Cleveland, the two went after a missed Jarrett Allen free throw. James inadvertently caught Edgecombe across the face with his elbow, leaving the 21-year-old guard with a bloody nose.

Edgecombe remained in the game with cotton in his nostril and helped the Sixers finish their comeback.

After the game, he was able to joke about the friendly fire.

"It's nothing bad," Edgecombe said. "But if it was, he can buy me a new nose."

Three games into James' Sixers career, the partnership has already produced points, a comeback win and a potential medical bill.

#4181. Pokémon Unite is shutting down in March 2027, days after its final tournament

📅 Oct 27, 2026  |  Category: World  |  Read Source →

The Aeos Crown will have a permanent winner, but everyone else is running out of time

Pokémon Unite’s biggest tournament of the year now has its finals booked for two days before the servers go dark. The Pokémon Company and TiMi Studio Group have announced that the MOBA is ending service on both mobile and Switch, so whoever wins the Aeos Crown 2027 in Tokyo gets to keep the title for good.

The esports side shouldn’t come as much of a shock if you were following along over the summer. Back in July, Pokémon confirmed Unite wouldn’t be part of the 2027 Championship Series or the World Championships, with the Tokyo event spun off as its own thing. So, August’s Worlds in San Francisco ended up being its last appearance there.

Then you’ve got the Aeos coins. They stopped being handed out last December, were swapped for License Journeys, and the coin exchange was wound down by May.

When you put two and two together, you’ll realise that the shutdown has been slowly arriving for about a year now. Pokémon Unite started off quite strong back in 2021. It just ran out of steam eventually, and I don’t think the Pokémon name was ever going to fix that on its own.

If you’re still playing, a fair bit changed this morning. Gem sales have stopped, as have new Unite Club memberships and automatic renewals, though any gems you’ve got can still be spent and current members keep their perks until their period runs out.

A send-off event and update land on November 12th, handing out Unite licenses and Holowear from across the game’s run, plus a library feature for looking back at certain records and in-game content. It only works if you download the data beforehand, or you can’t use it afterwards.

Pokémon Unite ends service on March 23rd, 2027.

#5416. At $13,337, Is This 2002 VW GTI 337 An Anniversary Gift You Would Give Yourself?

📅 Oct 27, 2026  |  Category: Business  |  Read Source →

Today's Nice Price or No Dice VW GTI is a special edition honoring the model's 25th anniversary and named—337—after its original internal code designation. That anniversary was almost 25 years ago, so we'll need to see if it's still just as special.

It's possible to go off "half-cocked." It's also quite possible to do a job "half-assed." That's often because enthusiasm for the task only rises to a "half-hearted" level. In my opinion, the worst half to be is "half-baked." Frustratingly, that just implies so much unrealized potential, or maybe some pretty weak weed.

General Motors is famous for producing half-baked products. Whether it was the original swing-axle Corvair or the Cadillac 8-6-4 gas engine two decades later, the company sometimes comes in hot and simply fails to stick the landing.

The 1978 Oldsmobile Cutlass Salon Brougham we looked at on Monday is an example of GM reaching for the layup and missing the basket. Its fastback body style was a loser with car buyers, something that should have been discovered in pre-launch focus groups, not by cars gathering dust in dealer showrooms. With its V8 engine and extremely rare five-speed stick, our Olds is a worthy survivor—a cautionary tale in the metal, as it were—but at $6,500, many of you were cautious about recommending its purchase. An overwhelming 73% 'No Dice' loss proved the result.

While it's not the first hot hatch, and potentially not history's best, when most people think of small, three- and five-door, performance-minded cars, Volkswagen's GTI is most likely the first to spring to mind.

When Volkswagen engineers first bandied about a tauter, more capable version of the company's bread-and-butter economy car, they assigned the project the internal code number 337. A production model resulted, debuting in 1976 for the European market and anointed with the now-legendary GTI name. North America had to wait seven more years to sample the GTI's wares, but it proved worth the wait. Flash forward a quarter century from that 1976 introduction to 2001, when VW decided to fete the model's 25th anniversary with a special edition, unceremoniously dubbed the 25th Anniversary GTI. All were painted silver, befitting the tradition of 25th-anniversary gift-giving.

Since that anniversary was irrelevant to North American GTI fans—and VW USA always seems a day late and a dollar short compared to its European sibling —that model got a pass. Instead, it was introduced as a 2002 model: the nearly same-spec 337 GTI, named for that original internal code, a nod to the depth of knowledge expressed by the marque's most rabid fans. Only 1,500 337s were made for North America in the 2002 model year, all, again, in Metallic Silver, although this time with less relevance for the color.

This 2002 VW GTI 337 appears to be an excellent, albeit not entirely original, example of this rare car. As with all 337s, it has the turbocharged 20-valve EA113 1.8-liter four under the hood. From the factory, that made 180 horsepower and 173 pound-feet of torque, enough to move the GTI's ton-and-a-half weight from zero to 60 mph in under six seconds.

Mated to that mill is a short-ratio six-speed manual actuated with a dimpled shift knob. Intended by Volkswagen to be a step up in features and capabilities over previous editions, the fourth-generation GTI was the first to get six cogs, and the first to offer a turbocharged gas engine. According to the ad, this 337 has been sprinkled with the Neuspeed fairy wand. Updates and additions include suspension components, an under-hood shock tower brace, turbo pop-off, and a cold-air intake. Apparently, the engine internals and turbo remain stock. The seller says the car runs without issue, sounds great, and gets amazing fuel economy, and that it "just purrs in sixth."

The car looks the part of a well-kept, loved family member with two notable exceptions. The first is on the exterior: a noticeable scrape on the front-right valance. That's a sad blemish on what's otherwise an almost perfect top coat. On the plus side, the BBS (yes, real BBS) wheels look to be in perfect shape and fill the wheel arches nicely when the car's lowered an inch or so. The ad mentions nothing, and no photo is close enough to read the date code on the Kumhos wrapping those wheels, so we don't know if a trip to the tire store is in this GTI's near future.

The other, and weirder, issue is in the cabin. The 337s all came with Recaro front buckets, and while this car has those Recaros in residence, with upholstery that matches the back bench, they are oddly embroidered with "GLI" on the backrests, not "GTI." As we all know, the GLI is the Jetta edition of the Golf's spin-off GTI. Nothing else is amiss in the cabin, and it all looks to be in good shape, though the driver's footwell floor mat does seem to have gone AWOL.

The ad notes the car has a modest 96,500 miles on the clock, comes with a clean title, promises a passing grade on the title transfer smog test, and includes mention of an available Carfax report showing it to be accident-free.

The seller calls the car "no nonsense," but then prices it at a badge-reflecting $13,337 asking. What we'll have to do is see whether or not we think that's a fair deal, or actual nonsense.

What's your take on this GTI 337 and that $13,337 price tag? Does that feel justified by the car's rarity, desirability, and presentation? Or is that too much even for the model's own upcoming 25th Anniversary?

The Cowboys got everything they could have hoped for on Monday in a get-right game against the two-time defending division champs, albeit a battered version of them, quarterbacked by backup Tanner McKee and led by Saquon Barkley on one leg with a hamstring injury. Prescott threw for 212 yards and three touchdowns. Javonte Williams totaled a career-high 150 yards rushing and a touchdown on 25 carries for a 6.2 yards per carry average. CeeDee Lamb led the way with 74 yards receiving and two receiving touchdowns on five catches while Pickens caught a 21-yard diving touchdown. For as beat up as the Eagles were, most of those injuries were on their offense. With Williams rolling, the Eagles had no answer for Prescott, Lamb, Pickens and Dallas' passing game.

* Dallas' most rushing yards against the Eagles since 2000

Edge rusher Nolan Smith (concussion) was the only Philadelphia defensive starter out on Monday, but a limited Barkley, no Jalen Hurts and no DeVonta Smith led to an environment where an outmatched Eagles squad couldn't keep pace with Dallas' high-flying, locked-in attack. The Cowboys should feel good about their ability to put Philadelphia out of their misery at Lincoln Financial Field. Oft-injured linebacker DeMarvion Overshown overcame a hamstring that had him sidelined since Week 1 to return Monday night in Week 7 to tally his first interception since 2024. Everything the Cowboys could have hoped for came to fruition while they were able to avoid an avalanche from Barkley, who finished with 62 yards and a touchdown on seven carries. That's before I even get into the fact that it weighs 315g, and when adding heavy wigs into the picture, you don't really want that stuck on your head for multiple hours at a time.

They're also Chroma-powered, so you can use Razer's Chroma app to sync them up to any other RGB-riddled Razer tech you have laying around.

However, typically, conventions and expensive tech you don't want to replace or have any harm come to them don't mix. No matter how purr-fectly cool and illuminating they look.
