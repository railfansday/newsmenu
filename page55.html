#1434. Gattuso: ‘Make Lazio fans proud, but with 11 players like me, you won’t win much’

📅 Oct 27, 2026  |  Category: Sports  |  Read Source →

Gennaro Gattuso is ‘proud’ so many Lazio fans made the trip for tonight’s Sassuolo match, hails Alberto Aquilani, but warns ‘if you have 11 Gattuso players, you won’t win much!’

It kicks off at the Mapei Stadium in Reggio Emilia at 17.30 GMT (18.30 CET).

You can follow all the build-up and action as it happens from this game, Roma-Cagliari and Torino-Como on the Serie A liveblog.

This is the first midweek round of the season, and therefore particularly unusual for a Lazio side who are not in Europe and crashed out of the Coppa Italia in the opening round.

Lazio fans are boycotting home games and have done for over a year now, but they flock to the away fixtures, so are here in numbers this evening, reported to be anywhere from 5,000 to 10,000.

“It makes us proud,” Gattuso told DAZN Italia.

“Naturally, we need to be ready, as Sassuolo are a team who play well, systematically have four attacking players and go on the attack very quickly.

“It’s a Tuesday early evening, we are grateful that they have come all this way to be by our side and hope to make them happy.”

It really will feel like a home game, because Sassuolo ultras announced that in protest against the kick-off time and cost of this midweek round, they will wait outside the stadium for the first 15 minutes of the match, urging others to do the same.

Gattuso was teammates with Alberto Aquilani at Milan in the 2011/12 campaign, his final season before hanging up his boots.

“I have a great rapport with him. Aquilani was always a very intelligent player and is carrying that on in his coaching career. He takes a lot of risks, is very positive, I feel like Sassuolo deserved more points than they currently have, and I am not surprised. I am sure he’ll have a great career.”

Lazio midfielder Reda Belahyane said this week that they all want to copy the example of Italy World Cup winner Gattuso when playing, but the coach joked about his lack of skill.

“If you have 11 Gattuso players in your line-up, you won’t win much! I think we are working hard, we show unity and play as a team, both when attacking and defending. If we keep that up, we can go far, but if we try to be fancy, don’t help each other out or lose humility, we will struggle. That is the secret.”

#2691. Kylian Mbappe apologizes to China as backlash reportedly forces state television to suspend LaLiga broadcasts

📅 Oct 27, 2026  |  Category: World  |  Read Source →

Kylian Mbappe apologized to China after a comment about a meme of himself sparked backlash, as Chinese state TV reportedly suspended LaLiga broadcasts.

Kylian Mbappe has apologized to China after a comment about a meme of himself drew backlash among Chinese fans. The statement, published on the Real Madrid forward’s social media accounts, comes as Chinese state television has reportedly suspended LaLiga broadcasts in the country.

The controversy began with a video, reportedly filmed in April and shared by the French podcast The Bridge, in which Mbappe was asked about his favorite meme. He said the funniest one he had seen put his face on “some guy,” adding “I think he’s a Chinese dictator”.

He did not name him, but the comment was widely read as a reference to a popular meme that places the forward’s face on an image of Mao Zedong, the founding leader of the People’s Republic of China.

“My comment was ill-judged and I now understand that it hurt many people in China and in Chinese communities around the world,” the French star wrote. He explained that he “reacted to an image of myself” and added: “I apologise to everyone my words have upset. This was absolutely not my intention. I have deep respect for the Chinese people and their history, and I am sincerely sorry”. The message was published in French, Mandarin, and English.

Backlash in China reaches LaLiga and Mbappe’s sponsors

According to The Paper, a state-backed Chinese outlet, state broadcaster CCTV suspended broadcasts of LaLiga matches and of games involving Mbappe starting Monday night, Oct. 26, citing sources linked to the public broadcaster.

The report also said all advertisements and videos featuring the forward were removed. The decision has not been officially confirmed by CCTV, and the league’s streaming deal with Migu, renewed through the 2029-30 season, has reportedly not been affected.

The reaction has also hit the player’s commercial deals. Beverage group Dongpeng, which said it has a “zero-tolerance policy” toward behavior that offends national sentiments, ended its contract with Mbappe with immediate effect, and dairy companies Mengniu and Milkground also cut ties.

China’s Foreign Ministry spokesperson, Guo Jiakun, said words and actions that hurt the feelings of the Chinese people “will naturally be met with joint opposition from all sectors of Chinese society”.

Javier Tebas apologizes on behalf of LaLiga

LaLiga has also responded to the controversy, issuing a statement in which the league “deeply regrets” the offense caused among Chinese fans and stressing that Mbappe’s comments “were made in a personal capacity and do not represent LaLiga”.

The organization apologized on behalf of the competition and all its clubs. President Javier Tebas went further in his own words: “We fully understand and respect the sensitivity of the Chinese people and sincerely regret any harm these statements may have caused,” he said, extending his apologies “to all those who have felt offended.”

Tebas highlighted the league’s long-standing ties with the country and warned against letting the episode damage them. “A relationship built over so many years cannot be damaged by a single episode,” he wrote on X, adding that LaLiga is “taking the measures within our reach and working directly with our partners and interlocutors in China to overcome this situation”. The league also announced it will develop a sensitivity and communication protocol together with clubs and players.

For 2027, Kia has handed the EV9 the smallest of price hikes, while boosting the desirable Land trim with more torque and a newly standard cosmetic package. I Drove the 2026 Kia EV9, and This is My Honest Review

The EV9 represents a major milestone for Kia. It delivers one of the most unique SUV designs in the industry, strong electric performance, and real three-row usability in a package that’s family-friendly and well executed.

Working your way up the trim ladder avails high-end features like a 14-speaker Meridian sound system, a heated steering wheel, power-adjustable second-row seats, heated/ventilated second-row seats, and the choice of a power sunroof or fixed glass roof.

We rated the EV9 GT-Line 9 out of 10 when we drove it earlier this year. This top trim may not be cheap, but the plush ride, potent acceleration, and huge interior make for a fantastic family vehicle. It does an equally competent job of keeping the driver and the kids happy.
