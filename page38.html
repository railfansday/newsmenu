Corning reported revenue of $436 million for its solar business in Q3 2026, a quarter-over-quarter decrease from the $438 million it reported in Q2 2026. Investors may find the drop in the solar business disconcerting, but given the company's otherwise strong quarter, it's certainly reasonable to consider picking up shares.
