#4246. Marathon launch player numbers are about half those of the Server Slam, at least on PC

📅 Mar 06, 2026  |  Category: Other  |  Read Source →

Marathon hasn't quite been able to attract the same volume of players the recent Server Slam did.

After a couple of delays, a plagiarism lawsuit, and at least one internal reboot, Bungie’s next big game has officially launched. Marathon, the extraction shooter from the studio behind Destiny, has managed to find a decent, if a little small of an audience.

The game’s player numbers on Steam are being compared to its recent free, open weekend, and not in a favourable manner.

Prior to Marathon’s launch, Bungie held a multi-day Server Slam, which offered anyone interested in playing the game a chance to try out almost all of its classes, two of its maps, as well as many of its gameplay and progression systems.

The open test was largely well-received, and players actually showed up for it. It peaked at a very respectable 143,621 concurrent players on Steam, which put it within striking distance of the peak concurrent of its chief competitor, Arc Raiders - at least when you compare both of their pre-launch Server Slam events.

In Arc Raiders’ case, those figures were far outpaced at the game’s actual launch, and the Embark shooter continues to command a very active player base to this day. A paid product garnering far more players than the same product’s own free sampler is never not a good sign, so many hoped something similar would happen with Marathon.

Marathon has now officially launched, and it doesn’t look like it managed to pull an Arc Raiders - at least so far. At its peak, the Bungie shooter attracted 88,337 concurrent players on Steam (via SteamDB), which is a little over half of its Server Slam’s 143,621 peak concurrent.

Numbers have, of course, dropped since then, which is normal, as players log off for the night. This is obviously not the end of the road for Marathon. Friday, and the weekend, may well push numbers closer to the Server Slam heights.

Indeed, word of mouth is quite positive, with the game currently sitting at a Very Positive user review rating on Steam, based on over 4,600 reviews. As has been proven time and time again in recent years, nothing catapults a game like players emphatically advocating for it to their friends, so it could simply be that mainstream players are cautious.

It will become clear where things settle over the weekend.

#4253. Diablo 4’s Warlock looks like an unhinged Necromancer with Sorcerer energy in first look at gameplay

📅 Mar 06, 2026  |  Category: Technology  |  Read Source →

Diablo 4's practitioner of forbidden arts, The Warlock, has been officially unveiled as the second class coming with Lord of Hatred.

Thanks to a meaty, well-produced Diablo 4 Developer Update, we got to have our promised first look at gameplay of the Warlock, the second of two classes coming to the game with the upcoming Lord of Hatred expansion.

The showcase offered a deep look at the Warlock’s core archetypes, its gameplay and skills loop, and where the class sits within the game’s existing roster of diverse classes.

Since the initial reveal of Lord of Hatred, The Warlock has remained the mysterious second class. Anyone who pre-purchased the expansion was able to get immediate access to The Paladin, the other class arriving with the expansion. It wasn’t until February that Blizzard officially revealed the class to the world, but we had to wait until this most recent Developer Update to actually see it in action.

The Warlock’s pragmatism exists as the counterpart to The Paladin's purity, in that Warlocks don’t shy away from harnessing the powers of Hell to eliminate their enemies. The class can conjure up demons to do their bidding, and utilise attacks previously only available to legions of Hell.

The Warlock is a hybrid pet and caster class. Their gameplay revolves around summoning demons, and undermining enemies with shadow arts. They rely on two resources: Wrath, and Dominance. Casting Skills consumes Wrath, whereas Dominance is what allows the Warlock to summon and exploit demons.

The Warlock has four archetypes players can spec into. Legions are traditional summoners who rely on overwhelming numbers to crush enemies. Vanguards are closer to Sorcerers, capable of dealing damage head-on. Masterminds utilise shadowcraft to slowly drain their enemies. Finally, Ritualists are summoners of the most terrifying Hell demons.

The Warlock is able to do this by fusing Soul Shards into their bodies. Going down any of the four paths becomes available after reaching level 30, which is when the class mechanic is unlocked. Each Soul Shard is tied to a certain demon, and its through that power that the Warlock is able to call upon them in combat. Your choice does, of course, affect your abilities going forward.

Warlocks arrive alongside new Uniques that cater to their specific playstyles, which is similar to what we’ve previously seen with The Paladin, and The Spiritborn before that.

Lord of Hatred arrives April 28. While we wait, Diablo 4’s next season is kicking off next week to deliver a bit of mindless fun to hold us over until the big day.

#4262. Diablo 4's next season is short, but it lets you become The Butcher, and it has Doom: The Dark Ages skins you probably can't afford

📅 Mar 06, 2026  |  Category: Technology  |  Read Source →

Blizzard has unveiled the next Diablo 4 season, which kicks off next week to hold us over until the launch of the game's big expansion in April.

The big Diablo 4 Developer Update has indeed delivered the goods. One of the livestream’s big topics was, of course, the game’s upcoming mini-season, which is set to kick off next week and run until the launch of Lord of Hatred, the game’s anticipated next expansion.

You can tell Blizzard wanted to have a bit of fun with Season of Slaughter, as many of its major features make it look like a limited-time event, but there is plenty to be excited about here.

Season of Slaughter kicks off March 11, and will run until the following season kicks off April 28, with the launch of the Lord of Hatred expansion. Many of the season’s biggest additions were available to preview in the 2.6.0 Public Test Realm.

Patch 2.6.0, as you might have guessed, is the one that will kick off the slaughter next week. The headline feature here is ability to become The Butcher, the iconic in-game boss who randomly invades players’ games.

When you play as The Butcher, you’ll be rewarded the more kills you get in quick succession. Your skills hotbar will transform to become something more fitting of The Butcher, making them more brutal. You’ll also earn a seasonal currency as you play.

Starting the seasonal questline is how you can begin to assume The Butcher’s form. A Taste of Power can be initiated from Gea Kul. Transforming into The Butcher can be done in a few ways. As you play, you’ll earn Meaty Offerings, which can later be spent on Shrines of Slaughter in the new Helltide. Once activated, the shrine will draw more enemies towards you.

If you never venture into PvP zones in Diablo 4, this season may change your mind. The standard PvP combat that could only be found in Fields of Hatred has been replaced with a Ceremony of Slaughter, a new event that rewards you for killing enemies quickly. See it through and The Butcher’s Idol will spawn, allowing you to transform into it.

Elsewhere in the world, you’ll also be able to attain the same power at Slaughterhouses, a new activity type available this season. Inside, you immediately become The Butcher for the entirety of the run.

The Butcher’s arrival also ties into two of the other Season of Slaughter’s more interesting additions. First, the Killstreak system finally arrives in Diablo 4, rewarding you for being as efficient as possible in carving your way through enemies.

Anyone who played Diablo 3 should be familiar with how it works. It kicks off the moment you get a kill, and has a visible timer where you’ll need continue performing the same action to up your streak. The more kills you get, the more often your timer will refresh.

There are five tiers to climb through: Killstreak, Carnage, Devastation, Bloodbath, and Massacre. You’re awarded for the tier you manage to reach before the timer runs out. You’ll earn XP and seasonal rep each time a Killstreak is achieved.

The other Butcher-themed addition is Bloodied Items, which is a new item quality available throughout this season. They play into the Killstreak system to grant you even more power. Bloodied Items come with unique affixes that scale off your Killstreak tier. You’ll see many familiar items in that pool, as any item in the game has a chance to drop as Bloodied. Bloodied Items can also be acquired by earning Fresh Meat.

Even if none of that sounds exciting, you’re probably going to redownload the game for The Paladin free trial, which kicks off alongside the new season on March 11. The trial is only available until March 18, and it lets you try out the Paladin class.

The trial is available across PC, PlayStation, and Xbox. You’ll be able to take the new class for a spin, but your progress will be capped at level 25. The Paladin can be permanently unlocked by buying the Lord of Hatred expansion, as it’s one of two classes the expansion is introducing.

The good news is that any progress you make in the free trial will carry over to the full game if/when you buy the expansion.

Season of Slaughter also kicks off a Doom: The Dark Ages collaboration event. As we’ve come to expect, there’s a very limited free component to the collaboration, in the form of the Doom Reliquary. This is essentially a mini-battle pass that includes the Shield Saw skin, Slayer’s Flair, the head of the Cyberdemon as a back trophy and various other smaller bits.

The more impressive items - such as character skins, will instead be available to purchase for real money. The list includes the Doom Slayer armor, Mecha Dragon Serrat skin for your cat mount, and a Cacodemon pet.

Though the armor skin is cross-class compatible, judging by how much previous collaboration items have cost in Diablo 4, most of us probably won’t be spending that kind of money to get it.

#4279. The Sims 4 is getting paid mods, and a new in-game currency to pay for them

📅 Mar 04, 2026  |  Category: Technology  |  Read Source →

The Sims 4 is about to launch a couple of major initiatives that may not exactly be what players have been looking for.

The Sims 4 has officially entered into its paid mods era. Developer Maxis announced The Sims Maker Program, and The Sims 4 Marketplace - two new destinations to publish, and discover new add-on content.

The two new features will arrive first on PC and Mac, starting March 17. PlayStation and Xbox players will receive them over the next couple of months, which is very much in line with how other franchises have rolled out their own paid mods systems.

The Maker Program is a way for community creators to get their work out there, and profit from it. The content they create will end up on the Marketplace, where other players can browse it, and pay for it if they want to use it in their game.

Created content will be available across all platforms. All previous packs The Sims 4 players should be familiar with will move to the Marketplace, too: once the feature launches, this is where you’re going to find Expansion Packs, Game Packs, Stuff Packs, Kits, and other official additions created by Maxis.

Alongside this, the game is also introducing a new virtual currency - Moola - which is what you’re going to use to purchase Maker Packs, as well as Sims 4 Kits in the Marketplace. A portion of your purchase will go to the creator (roughly 30%) of what you buy.

It is worth noting, however, that you’ll still be able to buy Expansion Packs, Game Packs, and Stuff Packs without having to purchase Moola, or go through the Marketplace. Kits are the only items that will no longer be available on the existing PC/console storefronts. Moola comes in the usual denominations you’ve seen a hundred items in other games’ in-game shops.

In order to start creating custom content and publishing it on the Marketplace, you’ll need to first be accepted into the program. Once that’s done, you’ll be able to directly publish your content on the Marketplace.

In the blog post announcing the new features, Maxis said that creators will have control over the price of their content, and how it's packaged. These are referred to as Maker Packs, which can include Create-a-Sim or Build/Buy items.

You can apply to become a Maker from March 5. Once accepted, you’ll also get access to the Maker Suite, which is what you’re going to use to test your work to ensure it runs smoothly on the different platforms, and eventually publish it.

#4378. Love and Deepspace ushers in the Year of the Horse with a Wuxia-inspired AU multi-banner and a restaurant management mini-game

📅 Feb 11, 2026  |  Category: Other  |  Read Source →

A new Love and Deepspace banner event is here with a lot of rewards to earn, and brand-new Memories for all five lads to unlock.

Love and Deepspace has kicked off the next big in-game moment and is celebrating Chinese New Year with the arrival of a new 5-Star Memory Series. This year's spring-themed banner is available now until February 27, and it comes with a host of events.

The Mortality’s Tenderness banner features five new limited-time 5-Star Memories, one each for the game’s five love interests. The theme is all about warmth, reunion, and intimacy, taking place in a Wuxia-inspired setting that's once again an alternate universe from the main story of the game.

The new memory cards are Xavier: Blades with Blossoms, Zayne: Entwined Kites, Rafayel: Carved Gemheart, Sylus: Shared Lanterns, and Caleb: Clearday Return. You can pick three of the five to focus on and further pick one to guarantee on your second 5-star pull; and you can earn wish rewards by making cumulative wishes over the course of the event.

Some of the rewards you could get include limited headwear, Limited Deepspace Wishes, and — of course — Memory-themed outfits and hairstyles inspired by the new Memories. Outside of wish rewards, the Memory Series comes with growth tasks that rewards you with in-game materials for levelling up the new cards during the event. When you reach Rank 1 for any new Memory from the banner, you’ll earn a Memory-themed outfit with a special colour that matches your chosen love interest.

Illusio returns as part of the new event, too, letting you customise the look of Kindled Moments across all five new Memories. Things you can customise include selecting the outfits and accessories for each love interest, so you can recreate Kindled Moments in your own style. As always, Kindled Moments will go back to their default look after the event ends.

There’s a new mini-game to play, too, available only during the event. It’s called Courteous Fare, a frantic but familiar restaurant management simulation that allows you to unlock a variety of rewards.

Furthermore, if you log in for 10 days throughout the event, you can unlock a new outfit set, Wealth Overflow, which can be worn by any of the love interests as well as the heroine. There are other login rewards, too, like 10 Deepspace Wishes, 10 Empyrean Wishes, His and My Outfit sets, two dynamic portrait photo poses, and loads more.

Finally, the Golden Fortune event adds even more daily log in rewards in the form of one free "shake" per day, from which you can earn a random reward of between 68 and 108 Diamonds — that adds up to a total of 1,296 Diamonds if you show up every day.

People seemingly haven't been able to stop playing Nioh 3 since the moment it came out, and everyone helped push the numbers to unprecedented heights for the series.

Nioh 3 has had a massive weekend on Steam, the type of launch you just don’t often see for Koei Tecmo games. Indeed, this has very clearly been a monumentous release for both the publisher, and developer Team Ninja.

It is also, quite visibly, the most popular Nioh release ever on PC, and it was impressive watching more and more players jump into the game over the weekend and push the numbers higher.

Nioh 3 came out swinging, breaking the series’ Steam concurrent player record immediately after lanch, with 42,822. Anyone keeping up with how well the game has been received, and how much it’s been anticipated by series fans and Soulslike players, would’ve told you that it was only the beginning.

Late on Friday, and several times on Saturday, the game continued achieving new records, setting new peak concurrent levels every couple of hours. It happened again on Sunday, when the game finally settled on its current 88,045 peak concurrent (via SteamDB).

That’s an incredibly healthy player base, but more than that, it’s more than double the previous record holder, Nioh 2, which peaked at 41,325 concurrent players in 2021. Steam players appear to be very happy with the game, too, with over 1,560 reviews and an overall Very Positive rating.
