RWE are in strong form and are looking for a third win in four games, though top-flight Die Roten Bullen remain the favourites as they hope to build on their victory at the weekend.

Uwe Koschinat's RW Essen have enjoyed a strong campaign in the 3. Liga and are in the hunt for promotion, currently sitting third in the table.

On Friday, RWE secured a 1-0 triumph over Jahn Regensburg, courtesy of a back-post header from Lucas Brumme with just 15 minutes on the clock.

That win means Koschinat's side have now lost just one of their last 14 outings across all competitions, winning 10 as part of an impressive run that goes back to a 5-2 defeat to Saarbrucken on August 9.

RW Essen reached this stage by beating St Pauli 2-0 in the first round on August 23, and overcoming the second-division club - who were relegated from the Bundesliga at the end of 2025-26 - will give the underdogs confidence ahead of Tuesday's showdown.

Additionally, the hosts are undefeated at Stadion an der Hafenstrasse throughout the entirety of this season, winning seven times and drawing just once.

Meanwhile, Leipzig have endured a tumultuous start to life under Martin Demichelis, who will see this clash as a gilt-edged opportunity to build form.

Die Roten Bullen thrashed Elversberg 4-0 on Saturday, a game that saw young striker Tidiam Gomis open the scoring early in the second half, before regular centre-forward Romulo bagged a brace off the bench either side of fellow substitute Christoph Baumgartner's third.

That success maintained the Saxons' excellent record at Red Bull Arena, where they have won all five of their matches this term without conceding a single goal, finding the back of the net 18 times in the process.

However, Demichelis's men have struggled immensely on the road so far, losing each of their five away fixtures since hammering Eintracht Trier 6-0 in the DFB-Pokal's first round on August 22.

Leipzig will be expecting to break that streak of defeats up against third-tier opposition, and triumph could provide the team with a psychological boost, though a shock defeat would be damaging for morale and will only serve to increase the pressure on Demichelis.

RW Essen are lighter in attack than ideal for this clash, given that strikers Semir Telalovic (shoulder) and Jaka Cuber Potocnik (ligament), as well as wingers Marvin Obuz (ankle) and left winger Aiman Dardari (muscle) are all expected to be unavailable.
