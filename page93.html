#3491. Apple's iMac with M6 arrives promising a 'huge leap' in AI performance

📅 Oct 27, 2026  |  Category: Technology  |  Read Source →

It offers more cores and more performance per core than the iMac M4.

After unveiling a new iPad mini yesterday, Apple is continuing its staggered "Pro and Tell" product release with the launch of its latest iMac model. Powered by Apple's shiny new M6 processors, it promises a "major boost" in performance and AI capabilities thanks to extra cores and new Neural Accelerators in each core.

The M6 first arrived in the Mac mini and Mac Studio, but it's now available in Apple's entry-level iMac desktop machines. As we saw before, the M6 chip offers not only more performance per core, but more cores in total with up to a 12-core CPU, 12-core GPU and Dual 16-core Neural Engine. That processor, along with faster 170GB/s memory bandwidth, will make the iMac twice as fast for graphics performance compared to the previous iMac M4 model, accelerating intensive tasks like photo editing and gaming, Apple said. Models start with 16GB of unified memory, with options for 24GB and 32GB.

Since it's mainly designed for productivity, Apple says the iMac is up to 50 percent faster for tasks like Microsoft Excel compared to the iMac M4, or 2.5x faster than the M1 model. However, these machines are plenty fast enough for more demanding jobs like photo editing and even video editing. It also delivers up to double the gaming performance for ray tracing, according to Apple.

As it did with the Mini and Studio models, though, Apple is focused on hyping the iMac's AI capabilities. There, the extra speed is much more pronounced with up to five times faster on-device AI performance for prompt processing in LM Studio compared to the M4 model, or 12 times the speed of the M1 model.

That AI performance is important, as the iMac with macOS 27 runs the next generation of Apple Intelligence with Siri AI, which Apple calls "profoundly more capable" than the old Siri. iMac users will be able to tap into that AI to do things like ask questions and look for information in emails and photos. Siri AI is also built into Spotlight, so you can find answers on any topic and ask follow-up questions. (Note that Apple Intelligence isn't yet available in Europe.)

The 4.5K 24-inch Retina display with 500 nits of brightness and P3 wide color is unchanged from before (we're still waiting for a new 27-inch iMac, Apple). However, iMac with M6 does have new Wi-Fi 7 and Bluetooth 6 capabilities, along with 2.5Gb Ethernet offering 250 percent more speed than before. Unlike the latest Mac Studio, it only supports Thunderbolt 4 rather than the two-times-faster Thunderbolt 5 connections, unfortunately. However, you can connect up to two 6K external displays if you love to have extra space to work.

Other features include a 12MP Center Stage camera and a selection of seven colors, plus color matched accessories and Touch ID. The new iMac with M6 goes on pre-order tomorrow (October 28th) starting at $1,499 with a 10-core CPU, 10-core CPU and 16GB of RAM, or $1,699 with a 12-core CPU and 12-core GPU (16GB of RAM).

#3714. Harry Potter HBO TV Series Casts James And Lily Potter, Hinting At Major Change From The Movies

📅 Oct 27, 2026  |  Category: Entertainment  |  Read Source →

Ahead of the premiere of "Harry Potter and the Philosopher's Stone" (the inaugural outing of HBO's Harry Potter TV series, which will set out to faithfully adapt each of the seven books about the titular boy wizard), we've gotten a steady trickle of casting news for future seasons, like Kit Harington's upcoming role as the fraudulent wizarding professor and author Gilderoy Lockhart. This latest update, though, is about the first season and shows just how the series will differentiate itself from the Harry Potter films ... by including characters and flashbacks we haven't seen before.

According to Variety and other outlets, the series, overseen by Francesca Gardiner (who previously worked on HBO's "Succession"), has announced three key roles for Season 1 that were previously kept under wraps. Harry Gilby will play Charlie Weasley, the second-eldest son of the wizarding family the Weasleys who works as a dragon tamer in Romania and helps Harry Potter (Dominic McLaughlin), Hermione Granger (Arabella Stanton), and his own younger brother Ron Weasley (Alastair Stout) deal with a dragon at one point. In addition, Jasper Talbot and Hannah Chinn will appear in "Harry Potter and the Philosopher's Stone" as James and Lily Potter, Harry's parents who are killed by the Dark Lord Voldemort when Harry is just one year old and approximately 10 years before the narrative properly begins. (Voldemort's casting, as of this writing, remains a secret.)

This is pretty big news to anyone who's familiar with the first Harry Potter book and movie. Why? Charlie, James, and Lily are all small but pivotal parts of the story, and Charlie isn't even in the first film in the Harry Potter franchise. Not only that, but this could right a major narrative wrong that concerns James and Lily in the movies.

It's wild, in retrospect, that Charlie Weasley never shows up in the Harry Potter film franchise. Not only are the Weasleys a huge part of the narrative — both because Ron is a main character and because they become a surrogate family to the orphaned Harry — but every single other Weasley gets an on-screen appearance. The powers that be, though, decided to cut Charlie from the first movie, "Harry Potter and the Sorcerer's Stone" (which retained the title "Harry Potter and the Philosopher's Stone" in most territories around the world), which means he also doesn't appear in the fourth film, "Harry Potter and the Goblet of Fire," during another dragon-centric storyline where he pops up in the books.

As for James and Lily, if you look closely during a scene in "Harry Potter and the Deathly Hallows — Part 1" where Harry and Hermione (Daniel Radcliffe and Emma Watson in the movies) go to visit Harry's parents' graves in Godric's Hollow, you can see their gravestone clarifies that they were both born in 1960. Canonically, the entire narrative of the Harry Potter books takes place in the 1990s. Harry himself was born in 1980, which means James and Lily were barely 20 years old when they had their son, but in the movies, they're seriously aged up in all the flashbacks. While this makes them look more like stock-footage parents, I guess, it's completely wrong when it comes to the actual text. Jasper Talbot and Hannah Chinn are both decidedly on the younger side, so it looks like the series will right this small wrong.

"Harry Potter and the Philosopher's Stone" starts airing on HBO and HBO Max on Christmas Day 2026.

#1732. Every English player to win the Ballon d'Or - and how they won it

📅 Oct 27, 2026  |  Category: Sports  |  Read Source →

By Joshua Cole | 27 Oct 2026 13:23

Harry Kane has joined an exclusive group of English footballers after winning the 2026 Ballon d'Or, becoming the fifth Englishman to claim the prestigious individual honour and ending a 25-year wait for a winner from his country.

The Bayern Munich striker was crowned the world's best men's player at the London Palladium on Monday, October 26, following a remarkable campaign that brought domestic silverware and international recognition.

Kane finished ahead of Kylian Mbappe and Lamine Yamal, adding football's most coveted individual prize to a career defined by prolific goalscoring.

Kane's triumph also provides a fitting backdrop to the ceremony's return to England, 70 years after Blackpool winger Sir Stanley Matthews became the inaugural recipient in 1956.

However, the journey between the two English winners has been anything but straightforward, with only three other compatriots claiming the award in the intervening decades.

Here, Sports Mole looks at the five Englishmen who have won the Ballon d'Or, the achievements that earned them recognition and the legacies they left on the game.

Matthews set the standard when he won the inaugural Ballon d'Or in 1956, edging Real Madrid legend Alfredo Di Stefano by three points and finishing ahead of Raymond Kopa.

The Blackpool winger's award recognised his exceptional individual ability during the 1955-56 campaign, when he helped the Tangerines finish second in the First Division, their highest league position in 25 years.

Renowned for his dribbling, balance and ability to beat defenders, Matthews earned a reputation as one of the most exciting wide players of his generation.

His victory was particularly remarkable because he was 41 years old, making him the oldest Ballon d'Or winner in history.

Matthews' achievement established an English connection to the award from its very beginning, and he was later knighted in 1965, becoming the first footballer to receive that honour while still playing.

Although he never won the First Division title or the FA Cup, his individual brilliance and longevity secured his place among the game's pioneering superstars.

A decade passed before another Englishman lifted the trophy, with Charlton's triumph coming in the same year that England won the World Cup on home soil.

The Manchester United midfielder finished just one point ahead of Benfica forward Eusebio, with Franz Beckenbauer third.

Charlton's influence on England's historic tournament was central to his victory, as he scored three goals, including both strikes in the 2-1 semi-final win over Portugal.

He was also named in the Team of the Tournament after helping England defeat West Germany 4-2 after extra time in the final.

A survivor of the 1958 Munich air disaster, he had rebuilt his career to become one of Manchester United's defining figures, later helping the club win the European Cup in 1968.

His standing in football history rests on his combination of technical quality, powerful shooting and remarkable resilience.

Charlton remains one of England's most revered players, and his 1966 triumph remains inseparable from the country's greatest international achievement.

Keegan became the third Englishman to win the Ballon d'Or in 1978 while playing for Hamburg, before retaining the award the following year.

His first victory came after a period of outstanding performances for club and country, with his energy, movement and relentless work rate making him one of Europe's most influential attacking players.

He finished ahead of Austrian striker Hans Krankl in the 1978 voting, while Dutch forward Rob Rensenbrink was among the leading contenders.

Keegan's second triumph was particularly significant because he helped Hamburg win their first Bundesliga title since 1960.

He scored 17 league goals during the campaign, including 11 in the final 12 matches as the club surged towards the championship.

Unlike Matthews and Charlton, Keegan did not need a World Cup-winning campaign to establish himself as the best player in Europe, and he remains the only Englishman to have won the Ballon d'Or in consecutive years.

Keegan later became a prominent manager, but his European playing career remains the defining chapter of his legacy.

Owen's victory arrived 22 years after Keegan's second award, with the Liverpool striker becoming the fourth English winner after a spectacular 2001.

Liverpool secured the FA Cup, League Cup and UEFA Cup, with Owen playing a decisive role in their success.

His two late goals in the FA Cup final against Arsenal turned a 1-0 deficit into a 2-1 victory, while his performances throughout the campaign established him as one of Europe's most dangerous forwards.

Owen also scored a memorable hat-trick in England's 5-1 World Cup qualifying win over Germany in Munich, and finished ahead of Real Madrid's Raul and Bayern Munich goalkeeper Oliver Kahn in the Ballon d'Or voting.

At just 22, Owen became the first Liverpool player to win the award and one of its youngest recipients, with his explosive pace, intelligent movement and clinical finishing making him a feared striker, while his international career included appearances at three major tournaments.

Injuries ultimately prevented Owen from maintaining the extraordinary level of his early career, but his 2001 achievements remain among the finest individual campaigns by an English forward.

Kane's triumph ends the longest wait between English Ballon d'Or winners, with 25 years separating his success from Owen's in 2001.

The England captain's recognition represents the culmination of a career in which consistency, professionalism and an exceptional understanding of the centre-forward role have made him one of the most respected strikers of his generation.

His move to Bayern Munich in 2023 provided a new platform for his goalscoring, while his ability to combine finishing with intelligent link-up play has strengthened his standing in European football.

He scored 73 goals in 65 appearances for Bayern Munich and England, while he won the Bundesliga and German Cup trophies, while also scoring six times at the 2026 World Cup as England finished third.

The award also marks a significant milestone after years of individual excellence without the game's highest individual honour.

Kane previously experienced disappointment in major finals, including Tottenham's defeat in the 2019 Champions League final and England's losses in the 2021 and 2024 European Championship finals.

At 33, Kane's recognition is both a reward for sustained excellence and a defining moment for English football.

#3500. Apple has updated the 14-inch MacBook Pro with an M6 chip

📅 Oct 27, 2026  |  Category: Technology  |  Read Source →

The 16-inch version still runs on either the M5 Pro or M5 Max.

Hot off the release of the iPhone Duo and a big update to the iPad Mini, Apple is looking to keep its momentum going by refreshing the 14-inch MacBook Pro with a faster M6 chip.

Thanks to its new processor, Apple says the latest 14-inch MacBook Pro delivers 30 percent faster graphics compared to an M5-equipped model, along with a 25 percent boost to AI workloads. Other improvements include support for Wi-Fi 7 and Bluetooth 6 while still offering strong battery life with a runtime of up to 24 hours on a single charge.

The M6 chip inside the updated MacBook Pro features a 12-core CPU and a 12-core GPU with Neural Accelerators in each core, plus a dual 16-core Neural Engine and faster unified memory. In addition to general performance increases, Apple claims the M6's Neural Engine is 1.9x faster than before while "dramatically" accelerating AI tasks such as image generation, super resolution and upscaling. When using LLMs, the company says the 14-inch M6 MBP offers up to 1.3x faster time to first token performance and 1.3x faster rendering in Blender compared to an M5 model.

The new M6 MacBook Pro comes with 16GB of unified memory by default, but you can upgrade it to 24GB or 32GB for an extra $200 or $400, respectively. Storage starts at 1TB but can be expanded to 2TB for $500, or 4TB for $1,500. Other features include a Liquid Retina XDR display with up to 1,600 nits of peak brightness, a 12-megapixel Center Stage camera and faster charging when paired with something like Apple's 96W power adapter.

You can also upgrade the M6 MBP's screen with an optional nano-texture finish for $150 to reduce glare and reflections. The Space Black version even comes with an anodized coating to better hide the appearance of fingerprints.

In addition to improved Wi-Fi and Bluetooth, the new model has a range of ports and connectivity options including HDMI, MagSafe 3, a total of three Thunderbolt 4 slots, an SDXC card reader and a 3.5mm audio jack. The one downside is that anyone considering a larger 16-inch MacBook Pro is still limited to previous-gen M5 Pro or M5 Max chips instead of newer M6 silicon.

The 14-inch M6 MacBook Pro starts at $1,999 and comes in two colors: space black and silver. It will be available for pre-order starting tomorrow (October 28), with officials sales slated to begin on November 6.

#5560. CenterPoint expands 10-year capital plan by $4B to handle load growth

📅 Oct 27, 2026  |  Category: Technology  |  Read Source →

Let Utility Dive's free newsletter keep you informed, straight from your inbox.

The Houston-based utility’s investment plan through 2035 stands at almost $71 billion, and the company is accelerating some investments to support growing customer demand.

CenterPoint Energy expects to see 14 GW of new demand online through 2030, and has added $4 billion to the utility’s 10-year capital investment plan, company officials said Tuesday in a third-quarter earnings call with analysts.

Total planned capital expenditures through 2035 stand at $70.7 billion, up from $66.7 billion the company announced in July. The company has also accelerated about $1.5 billion of capex into the first half of the plan, helping drive a $4.5 billion increase to planned investment through 2030.

Of CenterPoint’s planned capex, $51.5 billion is on the electric side and $19 billion will go towards the company’s natural gas operations. The remainder will occur at the corporate level.

“This incredible demand is creating a powerful multiplier effect, as large industrial expansions drive broader commercial growth across our service territory,” CenterPoint Chairman, President and CEO Jason Wells said on the earnings call. “Notably, industrial and commercial leasing activity in the Houston area is up more than 30% from last year.”

CenterPoint has received nearly 1 GW of distribution-level load requests in the last year, he added.

The 14 GW of expected demand growth are expected to generate $5 billion in customer savings across Texas over the next 10 years, company officials said.

“To replenish that capacity and sustain the region's competitive advantage for the next wave of demand, earlier this year we began an internal transmission study focused on increasing load serving capacity in three key areas,” Wells said, pointing to import transmission capacity, system stability and intra-regional transmission capacity.

Wells said CenterPoint has identified more than a dozen projects across these areas where investment is needed to add incremental load serving capacity. “This capacity is needed in the near term, as we already have requests for significant additional demand that we anticipate submitting in Batch One next year,” Wells said.

CenterPoint has submitted 17 GW of projects to the Electric Reliability Council of Texas’ large-load interconnection process and expects 12 GW to be eligible as base load or studied load. The first set of projects to navigate the new process is called Batch Zero, and a Batch One will be opened for applications in summer 2027.

Beyond the utility’s $4 billion capex increase, “we continue to see at least $10 billion of additional investment opportunities through 2035,” driven by transmission infrastructure to support Houston’s growth, resiliency efforts and large load opportunities in Indiana, Wells said.

“These incremental opportunities, combined with the investments announced today reinforce our confidence in delivering sustained double-digit rate-based growth over the course of our 10-year plan,” Wells said.

The utility reported net income of $331 million in the third quarter, or 50 cents/diluted share, compared with 45 cents/diluted share in the same period of 2025. And it extended its expectation for non-GAAP earnings per share growth at the mid-to-high end of its 7% to 9% annual range through 2030.
