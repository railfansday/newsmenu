#2924. Ex-NBA guard Malik Beasley to plead guilty after involvement in gambling scheme

📅 Oct 27, 2026  |  Category: Sports  |  Read Source →

Longtime former NBA guard Malik Beasley has agreed to plead guilty to federal charges related to illegal sports gambling, his attorney told both The Athletic and ESPN on Tuesday.

Beasley, who was arrested earlier this summer, will officially enter his plea on Dec. 9 in Brooklyn.

Beasley was indicted in June on federal charges related to a sports betting scheme alongside former NBA veteran Ed Davis. The duo, who played together for the Minnesota Timberwolves, were accused of working together to manipulate Beasley's performance in four games during the 2023-24 campaign. Beasley was on the Milwaukee Bucks at the time, and Davis was out of the league.

Beasley reportedly owed millions of dollars in a gambling debt, which led to Davis loaning Beasley money. But in order to pay off that debt, Beasley and Davis reportedly decided to launch a betting scheme. They reportedly worked with three others to place tens of thousands of dollars on a game that Beasley played in against the Cleveland Cavaliers that season. Beasley ended up scoring just three points in that game, well below his season average of 11.3 points per contest. Beasley is also accused of rigging a game against the Charlotte Hornets in similar fashion that same season.

Beasley has not played in the league since the 2024-25 season, which was one of the best in his career. He averaged 16.3 points per game, his best output in years, and finished in second in the Sixth Man of the Year race while with the Detroit Pistons. He was due for a big payday after that season, too, but his free agency was interrupted both by a federal gambling investigation and an NBA investigation into Beasley. He went unsigned, and hasn't played since.

Prosecutors alleged that Davis was in charge of the scheme. They were two of six men indicted in the scheme.

Beasley is the second active NBA player to plead guilty to charges related to gambling in the NBA. Jontay Porter was the first, and pleaded guilty in July 2024 amid a separate but similar scheme. The NBA banned him for life in 2024. It's unclear if Beasley will face that same fate.

#4792. Robinhood Taps Ripple's Stablecoin, Treasury Software and Trading Loans

📅 Oct 27, 2026  |  Category: Technology  |  Read Source →

Robinhood Taps Ripple's Stablecoin, Treasury Software and Trading Loans

Robinhood, the trading app with 28.6 million funded customers, is adding a digital dollar built by Ripple, the company whose founders created the XRP cryptocurrency. Ripple's RLUSD token went live Tuesday on Robinhood's own blockchain and on its Bitstamp exchange, and is expected to reach the main Robinhood app later this year, according to Ripple's announcement.

RLUSD is a stablecoin, a crypto token designed to always be worth one U.S. dollar, which lets money move across a blockchain without waiting for banks to open. It launched in December 2024, and Ripple said its market capitalization, meaning the combined value of every token in circulation, now tops $2.5 billion.

Robinhood will also run its global treasury operations on Ripple Treasury, software that manages a company's cash, from payments to cash-flow forecasts and risk. That includes intercompany netting, which offsets what a company's own divisions owe one another so fewer payments have to move.

Ripple Prime, a prime broker that lends money and clears trades for hedge funds and other large institutions, already provides Bitstamp with lending and margin financing, meaning borrowed money for trading, across roughly 20 digital assets. Ripple paid $1.25 billion for the firm in a deal announced in April 2025, when it was still called Hidden Road.

Ripple Prime clients also get access to spot trading on Bitstamp, which means buying at the current market price, and later this year Prime will support perpetual futures there, contracts that bet on a coin's future price and never expire. Robinhood closed its purchase of Bitstamp, one of the world's longest-running crypto exchanges, in June 2025 for about $200 million, and most of the exchange's volume comes from institutions.

Robinhood will use RLUSD on the XRP Ledger, the blockchain behind XRP, for institutional settlement, the step where a large trade is finalized and paid for, across Bitstamp's institutional business.

“Utility is what moves stablecoins forward. Working with Ripple, Bitstamp and Robinhood will have access to RLUSD for institutional settlement, Ripple Treasury for our operations, and Ripple Prime for financing,” said Johann Kerbrat, senior vice president and general manager of Robinhood Crypto and International. “That is what a connected, global financial system looks like.”

Robinhood, which pared back its crypto listings during the SEC crackdown on Ripple and other crypto companies, added XRP for U.S. customers in November 2024. Now Ripple's software will help run Robinhood's own treasury department.

Robinhood cut about 10% of its staff in June, after revenue fell 34% in its crypto transaction business in the first quarter to $134 million. It will report its third-quarter results after the market closes on Tuesday, the first report to cover a full quarter since the July 1 launch of Robinhood Chain.

#3191. ‘They Called The Police On A Three-Year-Old’: Family Says Toddler Was Kicked Out Of Youth Soccer League For Being Too Good

📅 Oct 27, 2026  |  Category: Sports  |  Read Source →

A video recently went viral in which a Black toddler and his parents appear to be kicked out of a youth soccer game. According to the child's parents, the three-year-old was kicked out of a youth soccer league for being better than his older peers. Police and the soccer league have commented on the incident, which is drawing outrage online.

The soccer incident was partially captured on video that has circulated widely across social media. In the first half of the viral clip, the young child, whose name is Nevada, can be seen dominating his peers during a soccer game at a park, easily outrunning and scoring against the other children as his dad, who is recording, cheers. The clip then cuts to an encounter between the young child's parents and police, who inform them that their family has been asked to leave the park. "They called the police on a three-year-old, cause he's too good," Nevada's dad says. "They called the police on a three-year-old cause he has the ball for too long and nobody else could get the ball." Later, Nevada's mom talks to a police officer, saying that they had never bothered the other parents. The caption accompanying one of the posts sharing the video on X identifies the incident as happening at an i9 Sports league soccer game in Winter Park, Florida, also adding that most of the other children in the game were one to two years older than Nevada. The caption says Nevada and his family were "trespassed" from the field by the soccer league.

Journalist Philip Lewis later spoke to Nevada's mother for HuffPost. She asked to be identified only by her first name, Martasia. She confirmed that her son, who she says began dribbling a soccer ball at 10 months old and has a significant social media following, was playing in a league with 5-year-olds when the other children's parents became angry. "As soon as he started the game, they stopped the game," she explained. "Other parents, the director, and some of the coaches got in a circle, and I knew they were talking about Nevada, trying to figure out how to let us know that he can't play so the other kids could play." Martasia recounted her interactions with the director who was present. "When I walked up on them talking about that, the director said that everybody is paying money, so everybody should play. They were trying to find a reason why he can't play. Once I started speaking up for Nevada, they just called the police," Martasia said. She explained that the experience "really traumatized Nevada." She added, "He still wants to play soccer. He just doesn't want to go back to that place."

Winter Park police confirmed that they had responded to a dispute between a league official and the parents of one of the players at the league soccer game, with the officers informing everyone involved that the issue was a civil matter. A spokesperson for the league said, "We are aware of concerns being shared regarding an incident at a local i9 Sports program, and we take them very seriously. The respectful treatment and well-being of every child and family matter deeply to us." The statement added, "We have reached out to the family and have begun a review that includes speaking with those involved," and the league declined to "speculate or share unverified information." Martasia confirmed that her family had received a refund for the money they paid to the league. Nevertheless, she remains angry about the impact this incident has had on her son. "It's heartbreaking because now Nevada's saying he doesn't wanna go over there. He's scared that he did something wrong."

Online reaction to the viral incident has been strong, with many people complimenting Nevada's impressive soccer skills and criticizing the decision to involve the police in a dispute over playing time and the skills of children. While these reactions show significant support for Nevada and his family, his mother says the incident has shaken him, though he still wants to play.

#3389. Warren Buffett's Successor, Greg Abel, Has $114.4 Billion Invested in These 3 Tech Stocks. None Is a Pure-Play Artificial Intelligence (AI) Company.

📅 Oct 27, 2026  |  Category: Technology  |  Read Source →

Warren Buffett has a reputation for investing in businesses with recognizable brands, dependable cash flow, and durable competitive advantages. Although the legendary investor occasionally ventured into technology, he generally avoided businesses whose long-term prospects were difficult to predict.

That said, Berkshire Hathaway (BRKA -1.44%)(BRKB -1.01%) is entering a new chapter. Following Buffett's retirement as CEO at the end of 2025, successor Greg Abel has overseen changes to Berkshire's investment portfolio. Interestingly, some of the investment conglomerate's notable positions now involve technology companies positioned to benefit from artificial intelligence (AI).

Three businesses -- Apple (AAPL +0.93%), Alphabet (GOOGL +1.19%) (GOOG +0.84%), and Bending Spoons (BSP +0.73%) -- represent approximately $114.4 billion, or 31.4%, of Berkshire's estimated $364 billion investment portfolio.

Here's what makes these investments interesting: None of these companies is a pure-play AI business. Instead, each company has established business models that could become more valuable as AI adoption accelerates.

Let's examine what makes these companies attractive investments and how Berkshire's positions have evolved.

Berkshire Hathaway CEO Greg Abel. Image source: Getty Images.

Berkshire first started accumulating a stake in Apple in 2016. Buffett has praised Apple's brand loyalty, supported by its installed base of billions of devices, which helps the company generate heaps of free cash flow year after year.

Berkshire has reduced its Apple investment over the last couple of years. After considerable selling between 2024 and 2025, the firm's position has stabilized. According to Berkshire's second-quarter 13F filing, the company owned 227.9 million Apple shares, now valued at $79.1 billion.

Apple's appeal extends beyond AI. Its ecosystem of iPhones, Mac computers, wearable devices, and subscription services creates high-margin, recurring opportunities to monetize its global customer base.

Of course, AI can strengthen this ecosystem considerably. Through Apple Intelligence, the company is integrating generative AI capabilities into its devices and operating system. These features could encourage customers to upgrade their hardware while creating new opportunities to expand Apple's services business.

While Apple doesn't need to dominate the AI infrastructure market to benefit from the technology, it can use AI to make its existing products more valuable.

Berkshire initially purchased Alphabet stock during the third quarter of 2025, when Buffett was still CEO. Following his retirement, the Oracle of Omaha publicly acknowledged that he initiated the investment. However, the firm's position in Alphabet has grown under Abel's leadership.

During the first quarter of 2026, Berkshire increased its Alphabet stake from approximately 17.8 million shares to nearly 54 million. The buying continued during the second quarter following a $10 billion investment associated with Alphabet's private placement. Berkshire's position in Alphabet is valued at $33.8 billion, spread across 106 million shares in Class A and Class C.

Alphabet operates Google Search, YouTube, and Google Cloud. These properties collectively generate substantial revenue and cash flow for Alphabet and give the company several opportunities to monetize AI.

Although generative AI initially appeared to threaten Google's advertising business, Alphabet has figured out how to incorporate AI-generated responses directly into its search experience. As a result, the company is now a legitimate competitor to query-heavy chatbots from OpenAI and Anthropic.

Meanwhile, Google Cloud is benefiting from companies seeking infrastructure for developing and deploying AI applications. Alphabet develops its own AI models (Gemini) and specialized computing chips (Tensor Processing Units), giving it opportunities to participate in multiple layers of the AI value stack.

To comprehend just how big of an opportunity cloud computing is becoming, consider that Google Cloud's revenue grew 82% year over year to $24.8 billion during the second quarter. Meanwhile, the company boasted $514 billion in cloud backlog.

Although Alphabet remains an advertising and cloud computing business, AI is strengthening those operations rather than requiring investors to bet on the success of a single AI product.

Berkshire's investment in Bending Spoons might be its most unconventional technology bet. Unlike Apple and Alphabet, Bending Spoons isn't a household name. The Italian company specializes in acquiring established digital businesses and improving their operations. Its portfolio includes recognizable brands such as Evernote, Vimeo, and AOL.

Berkshire initially invested $600 million in Bending Spoons during its initial public offering (IPO) in July. Earlier this month, Bending Spoons announced that Berkshire committed an additional $330 million through a private placement of 10 million shares priced at $33 per share. The investment increased Berkshire's ownership stake to about 4%.

Bending Spoons' strategy shares some similarities with Berkshire's own philosophy of acquiring businesses. However, Bending Spoons adds an interesting technology-enabled component: The company leverages AI to help optimize its acquired businesses. Rather than competing with OpenAI or Anthropic, Bending Spoons deploys data-driven services across its existing product portfolio.

These three investments illustrate an important distinction in the AI revolution: Investors do not need to own obvious AI names to benefit from AI adoption. Apple can use AI to strengthen its ecosystem, Alphabet can monetize AI through advertising and cloud services, and Bending Spoons can leverage the technology to improve its businesses' profitability.

For Berkshire, the common thread stitching these positions together isn't AI itself. It's the potential for established businesses to generate even more cash as technology evolves.
