#235. Melting glaciers in Dolomites reveal cache of first world war munitions

📅 Oct 27, 2026  |  Category: World  |  Read Source →

Italian army removes and defuses 250 explosives encased in ice for more than 100 years after hikers’ sightings

A significant cache of unexploded bombs from the first world war emerged this summer from rapidly retreating glaciers in the Dolomites, the Italian army has said.

The devices, which included Italian artillery shells and French mortar bombs, were removed and defused by the army’s Julia Alpine brigade, according to a statement.

The weapons originated from the white war, a battle between the Austro-Hungarian army and Italian troops across treacherous and bitterly cold terrain between 1915 and 1918.

More than a century later, hikers reported several sightings of bombs during the summer, mostly in the area of Canal San Bovo in the Primiero Valley in eastern Trentino. The soldiers then conducted an extensive sweep there and across other high-altitude and glacier locations in the Trentino Alto Adige region of the Dolomites.

They recovered and neutralised 250 explosives, including “18 Italian 75mm artillery shells and 37 Thevetot mortar shells – a French-made device that was also supplied to the Italian royal army during the first world war”, the Julia Alpine brigade.

It is not the first time that relics of the white war have emerged from melting glaciers, whose retreat has accelerated over more than decade.

“It is very much a consequence of global heating,” said Stefano Morosini, a professor at the University of Bergamo and the coordinator of heritage projects at Stelvio national park, where some of the explosives were also found.

“In recent years, at the end of each summer, these dangerous artefacts emerge and each year there is a higher number. They have been conserved by the ice for more than 100 years. Some had already exploded but many hadn’t, so now when they re-emerge, they are very, very dangerous.”

The advice to hikers and mountaineers is to “never touch any type of explosive device you might find, regardless of where it is located” and to promptly report any discovery to police.

Fought mainly in the Dolomites of Trentino Alto-Adige and the Alps of the Lombardy region, the white war was a period of history frozen in time until the 1990s, when global heating started to reveal an assortment of perfectly preserved relics, including weapons, sledges, letters and diaries.

A wooden barracks that had been dug into a cave on the top of Mount Scorluzzo, a 3,095-metre (10,154ft) peak overlooking the Stelvio pass, reappeared in 2015 after the ice which had sealed it off for almost a century completely melted.

The shelter had been hastily locked up when the first world war ended in November 1918, and the soldiers abandoned most of their belongings. Beds made of straw as well as clothes, lanterns, newspapers, postcards, coins, and tinned food were found well preserved inside.

A white war museum to host the relics is scheduled to open in Bormio in June 2027.

The white war is thought to have claimed more than a million lives, although the majority of soldiers were believed to have been killed by avalanches, falling down mountains or hypothermia.

Dozens of corpses, some still in their uniforms, have emerged from the melting ice over the past 15 years.

The relatives of an Italian soldier were able to bury him in 2017 after documents revealing his identity were found with his body on Presena glacier.

Morosini said the intense summer heat was making access to the glaciers in the Dolomites less safe. Eleven people died when a huge mass of the Marmolada glacier broke off in 2022.

“It is becoming more and more impossible to access the glaciers during the summer because of the very long heatwave at high altitude,” he said.

“If you come to the area, you can see how fast the glaciers are retreating and how strong the impact of climate change is.”

Radisson’s net zero push is moving from hotels it leases to hotels it manages. In South Africa, the hardest part wasn’t the building investments but buying the necessary renewable power.

Radisson has unveiled its first net zero hotel in Africa — and the owner, rather than the brand, is paying for it in expectation of a quick payback from higher rates.

The 138-room Radisson Safari Hoedspruit, near Kruger National Park in South Africa, is the group’s 10th Verified Net Zero Hotel, following four other recent additions, and one of the first managed properties in the program.

Verified Net Zero is Radisson’s own program that is independently verified by certification organization TÜV Rheinland. It requires hotels to use renewable energy, through electrification and renewable power purchases, to cut Scope 3 emissions, and to cover what’s left with carbon removal credits.

Until now, Radisson’s net zero hotels have mostly been leased properties, with costs on the group’s own books. Radisson plans to extend the scheme to more managed and franchised hotels from next year, as it works toward 100 Verified Net Zero Hotels by 2030.

For now, let's start with the basics and dive into our first Illusion Connect: Re tip.

You probably know that you can level up any character you want - well, the downside is that resources are limited, and I strongly recommend focusing only on your core team instead of levelling everything up.

I know it's tempting, but focusing mainly on your best SSRs (a tank, a DPS, and maybe a secondary DPS or a Healer - depending on what characters you have) is the best course of action.

If you rerolled, you probably have Miyuki or Yuffie, who are great early on. Invest most of your resources to level them up to max, and then focus on your tank. If you happened to pull Anna from the gacha, then you can also level her up. She's a great character to use in the Arena as well, so it's not a waste of resources.

Illusion Connect Re isn’t a passive auto-battler. Yeah, you can turn on the Auto function, but the AI doesn't always work in your favour. Auto will most often deploy a character you don't want, unless you only have 4-5 characters on your team and you're happy with the order.

The main reason I mention the order here is that Energy is an important resource, and if you use it all to deploy the characters in the wrong order, they don't return to your hand (unless they have a skill that specifically does that).

I cannot tell you the right rotation here, because it all depends on what characters you have and what Leader you're using, but as a rule of thumb, you always want to use a tank that has Taunt before your main DPS source (like Miyuki, Rotania, or Yuffie).

As you probably know, you can change your Leader once you meet the requirements to unlock the option. The Sakura Swordsman is a solid option, and even if it's unlocked by default, it can be used all the way into the late game (if you meet the requirements to unlock all of his skills).

As you can see, you will need to clear the Collapsed Dreams stages as much as you need to clear the regular Dream stages.

The story mode is the backbone of your early progression. Every chapter unlocks new features, resources, and ways to make your team stronger. I recommend you try to clear as many Dream stages as you can, and then you switch to Collapsed Dreams.

You can get a lot of Diamonds for clearing the stages with 3 stars, so I suggest you put Auto battle on hold when the stage is too hard, and try to clear it manually.

The Illusion Connect: Re home system looks like pure decoration, but it adds to your long-term progression. Characters have some interactions there, but most importantly, furniture pieces offer passive bonuses. In short, the whole system becomes a steady way of getting resources.

Even if you’re not the type who enjoys decorating, upgrading the home pays off. It's also cheap to do, and whenever you log back into the game, you should clear the rewards from there (it's also needed to complete one of the daily quests).

Illusion Connect: Re has a pretty generous list of gift codes, and they’re absolutely worth using as soon as you start. You will get a lot of currency and summons, and they will be a huge help in the long term. You will even get gear boxes, which are extremely important if you want to progress faster (those extra stats help a lot).

There are plenty of Diamonds to be claimed while you have story stages to clear, but once you finish them all, you will need another source of Diamonds. That's where the daily and weekly quests come into play. Make sure to clear them all regularly - it only takes 20 minutes or less, so it's worth it.

Since we're on the topic of Diamonds, you should save them all for exclusive pick-up banners that have characters that you need on your team. You don't have to do that from the start, though, so you can have fun with the gacha system (until you unlock some SSRs).
