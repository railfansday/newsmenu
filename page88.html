Sheffield United stunted their progress with a dismal summer ahead of the 2025/26 campaign. Not only did SUFC’s move to appoint Ruben Selles backfire heavily, but their player recruitment didn’t have much fruit to bear either.

Among the arrivals was young midfielder Alex Matos, who signed from Chelsea on a three-year contract. A tough debut campaign at Bramall Lane would follow, and the 22-year-old was left on the fringes before a summer escape route arose.

Matos is now approaching four months since his Blades departure. He will hope it can be a fruitful campaign as he looks to get back to his best after a challenging year.

Matos joined Sheffield United off the back of two promising loans with Oxford United and Huddersfield Town. After impressing in academy football with Chelsea, he stepped into the senior game with an impressive work rate and plenty of energy in the middle of the park.

However, the Bedford-born prospect would play only eight times for the Blades, with all of those appearances coming before the turn of the calendar year. Matos was named in just two Championship matchday squads in 2026 as he struggled to impress at Bramall Lane.

Goztepe offered him an escape route in the summer, striking a loan deal with an option to buy. Matos has found plenty of opportunities since, playing in seven of the Turkish club’s nine Super Lig games. He even netted on his debut – a 3-3 draw with Samsunspor.

Matos has started four times and been used off the bench on three occasions. His minutes have been more limited since the return after the international break, but he has shown he can be a useful asset for Goztepe.

The SUFC loanee is averaging 6.33 defensive contributions per 90, and six duel wins (Fotmob). His heatmap shows he is picking up the ball all across the pitch, covering plenty of ground and offering a passing option while also guarding the backline.

For now, Matos’ full focus is on impressing at Goztepe. He has plenty of potential to fulfil and will be eager to kick his development into action after a challenging year at Sheffield United.

The former Chelsea prospect will battle to lock down a starting spot and really prove his value ahead of a potential permanent move. If he can, you have to think the Super Lig side will take up the option to sign him from SUFC on a full-time basis.

Even if they don’t choose to do so, it feels likely that Matos’ long-term future lies away from the Steel City. Other clubs will be sure to track his progress in case Goztepe pass up on the chance to keep the England youth international.

#2582. EXCLUSIVE | Gilles Grimandi on Arsène Wenger: ‘What he passed on to me was his passion for football.’

📅 Oct 27, 2026  |  Category: Sports  |  Read Source →

Get French Football News sat down with former Arsenal FC and AS Monaco star Gilles Grimandi, who paid tribute to his former Arsenal teammate Robert Pires and his coach at both Monaco and Arsenal, Arsène Wenger.

There were around 100 players present at Robert Pires’ testimonial earlier this month. Do you think that says a lot about Robert Pires himself, both as a player and as a person?

Yes, I was at the testimonial. It was a fantastic celebration. I think it was one of the most successful testimonials in a very long time. The welcome from the public and the locals was surprisingly fantastic. Above all, Robert managed to bring a lot of people together, and that allowed us to go back in time for a little while. We got to meet people we hadn’t seen for a very long time. There was an atmosphere of kindness and sharing that really warmed the heart during this rather difficult period we’re currently going through in France. It was a really wonderful event, and it reflected Robert himself: he’s always kind and considerate. I think Metz contributed greatly to the success of the occasion, both through the welcome they gave everyone and through the people in the stands.

It says everything about Robert because if you don’t have a good relationship with someone, you don’t go to their testimonial. You don’t take part in an event where that person is the main focus. Robert isn’t someone who creates conflict; he’s someone who enjoys life. He’s an epicurean. Robert loves life, enjoys good food and likes people. He doesn’t have any bad intentions, so I think everyone likes him as a person. A large part of the reason people came was Robert himself.

There were some transport and scheduling difficulties, so people had to make an effort to get there. If they made that effort, it’s because Robert has that quality of kindness. That was certainly the feedback from people in France about the event. I was surprised by how widely it was watched; lots of people had spoken to me about it, and everyone loved seeing so many people together again and experiencing the friendly atmosphere. I’m very pleased.

It was a great occasion for Metz, certainly. We saw Carlo Molinari [legendary Metz ex-President] and people who are part of the history of French football. It was a real pleasure to meet them. Joel Müller [Metz coach under whom Pires played] was there too. It was a really, really lovely evening.

The Arsenal manager at the testimonial was, of course, Arsène Wenger. You have a close connection with him from Monaco and then Arsenal. What influence did he have on you personally, on Arsenal and perhaps even on English football in general?

For me, his influence was obviously enormous. I played under him for nine years, then worked with him in recruitment for fifteen years. I think what he passed on to me was his passion for football. It’s deeply rooted in him; it’s in his blood. He loves the game profoundly and is passionate about it – perhaps excessively so, perhaps too much at times. That made certain periods difficult for him. He has such a strong desire to win and succeed, and he puts so much commitment into it that it’s difficult when things don’t go the way he wants, given how much he invests.

He passed that passion on to me. When you work alongside him, he’s everywhere because he’s so passionate and doesn’t miss matches. You have to be able to respond constantly and try to meet his demands. I only ever knew football in that way: through total commitment. Sometimes, when I see people entering this profession, I think they need to understand that you’re effectively giving your life to football. Otherwise, you can’t succeed. That’s the main thing Arsène taught me.

As for English football, I think there were two particularly interesting factors in Wenger’s arrival in England. Arsène had a broad, overall vision of football, while the English had a very specific focus on competition. They were focused on Saturday at three o’clock. Those two approaches came together. Perhaps in French football we didn’t always handle competition particularly well. Maybe we put too much pressure on the occasion and made it too important, whereas the English could enjoy the event and keep it in perspective while still giving their maximum. I think they had a very good approach to competition, perhaps better than we did in France.

On the other hand, they lacked that broader vision in areas such as nutrition, medicine and related matters. Arsène brought structure to all of that, and it helped the club succeed. But success is always validated by trophies. If there hadn’t been trophies, perhaps Arsène ‘s project wouldn’t have worked – or people would have said it hadn’t worked – even though the ideas were there. Unfortunately, many people bring new things to football but don’t get recognition because they don’t achieve success. Guardiola’s case is similar: people validate his project because he has succeeded and won trophies. Without trophies, it wouldn’t be validated. Arsène was fortunate, too, to work with English players who contributed something to his project.

#1471. Allegri: ‘Given up on the appeal, might end up with a four-game ban instead of two’

📅 Oct 27, 2026  |  Category: Sports  |  Read Source →

Massimiliano Allegri has confirmed that he has ‘given up’ on his appeal of the two-match suspension that he was given after Napoli’s recent 3-3 draw against Roma, and has identified where his side need to improve heading into Wednesday evening’s game against Monza in Serie A.

Allegri sat down for his customary pre-match press conference ahead of Napoli’s upcoming Serie A match against Monza, which kicks off at 19.45 GMT on Wednesday evening.

During his talk with the press, Allegri discussed a number of topics including his touchline outburst over the weekend, which has earned him a two-match suspension, his side’s need to improve defensively and the fitness of Scott McTominay, Andre-Frank Zambo Anguissa and Antonio Vergara.

The first questions that came Allegri’s way referred to his red card at the end of Napoli 3-3 Roma and the fiery comments that Allegri was reported to have made in the tunnel.

“There’s not much more to say, so much has happened now. This morning, I decided to give up on the appeal. You never know, I might end up with a four-game ban instead of two,” Allegri joked.

“The biggest regret isn’t the suspension, but the fact that we didn’t bring home the three points after playing a good game. The rest is all talk. It’s a case of would have/should have. Not what he did say, but what he might’ve said. Let’s have a laugh about it now.

“I’m sorry I won’t be there in the dugout. I enjoy going to Turin. Those who will be there will do a better job than me, I have a great staff.”

When Gian Piero Gasperini spoke at a press conference on Monday, he said that the way the incident unfolded and the way it was reported was ‘unfortunate’.

Asked for a response, Allegri said: “I didn’t hear what he said, but the words that were reported might’ve been wrong. But we can’t change the result of the suspension now. We have to work hard, we’re behind in the standings and all we can do is work hard to try and improve it, to improve on certain in-game situations where we’re letting ourselves down at the moment.

“Maybe in a month, things will have changed, but we have to work on the performances. The players’ conditions are improving. Several have been out for two months like McTominay, Anguissa; Alisson has been in and out, Neres has been injured, now Badiashile. We have to get those who are out back in and improve the condition of those guys who have played less.”

Allegri began to dissect his side’s recent 3-3 draw against Roma and the lessons that he has learned from that outing.

“We had a good game against Roma. We need to do better in terms of distribution. At times, we forced it against Roma when we could have managed possession better. At 2-0, we should have controlled it better. In the first halves, we have only conceded two goals this season. In the second halves, we’ve conceded eight, so there’s data that shows us where to improve and that helps us understand that we need to be better in certain moments.”

Allegri was asked about his recent tactical tweaks and whether this has been the reason behind Napoli’s improved performances over the last week.

He said: “If Lobotka plays in front of the defence, he has the right characteristics for it, he’s an excellent player. If Gilmour plays there, it’s different, with De Bruyne, it’s more direct, he can change the game in an instant. It’s a matter of characteristics. They give you different ways of playing. De Bruyne is extraordinary at playing these 50-yard passes, but he can’t play every single game like some of the others, so changes become fundamental.”

And what about Andre-Frank Zambo Anguissa, who played the full 90 minutes against Roma over the weekend, but might need his minutes managing after recent injury issues.

“The only way to get them back to their best condition is to let them play,” Allegri insisted. “He’s been rested since last year, he’s not played enough.”

There were also updates on Amir Rrahmani and Antonio Vergara: “Rrahmani has returned to the group, but he’s been out for many months. We need to be good but also a little lucky to get results and rehabilitate players at the same time.”

And on Vergara, he added: “He’s available, Antonio has excellent qualities. He’s a winger who loves to come inside, a raider, he likes to get into the box. He’s got seven or eight goals in him in a league season. He’s got his own characteristics, he has to improve in some areas of the game in terms of tactics and individual technique.”

And how about Rasmus Hojlund, who has put in some selfless performances of late but is without a goal himself for the last eight games at club level. Is there a chance that Lorenzo Lucca replaces him at centre-forward?

“Lucca will definitely have an opportunity to play, he had an excellent pre-season, he’s scored goals. He might feel the pressure a bit more, but he’s given us his availability. Against Genoa, he came on and provided the action that led to the goal. We have to be confident of his qualities.

“Hojlund is very strong physically and now he’s taking games to the very end, not falling short in the 60th minute. He had three big chances the other day, but did well.”

Lastly, Allegri was asked about his side’s defensive output. Despite a win and a draw over their last two matches, Napoli have conceded six in those games and have shipped 10 in their last four games, letting in three goals in three of those games.

Allegri said: “I’ve seen some data. We’re conceding an average of four shots on target per game, but that doesn’t tell you how we concede them or where we’re conceding them from.

“The data says we concede four shots on target, so we have to work on ourselves when we are defending our own area, like for Soule’s first goal or the third goal against Roma. We have to work on that, there will be some mistakes, we will be better at other times.”

Peter Young is a Senior Reporter with Football Italia.

#2542. Bayern Munich’s Aaron Danks admits ‘English bias’ in celebrating Harry Kane’s Ballon d’or triumph

📅 Oct 27, 2026  |  Category: Sports  |  Read Source →

Bayern Munich assistant coach Aaron Danks has congratulated his fellow Englishman Harry Kane after the 33-year-old striker was crowned the world’s best footballer at Monday evening’s Ballon d’Or ceremony in London.

Speaking at Tuesday’s pre-match press conference ahead of Bayern’s DFB-Pokal encounter against 1. FC Magdeburg, Danks expressed his delight at Kane receiving football’s most prestigious individual award.

“A fantastic moment for him and a great achievement,” Danks said of Kane’s Ballon d’Or triumph.

“Of course, as an Englishman, I’m not entirely neutral,” Danks continued. “I’m delighted for him that he’s received recognition for all his hard work.”

Kane became the first Englishman to win the Ballon d’Or since Michael Owen in 2001, having enjoyed an outstanding 2025/26 campaign with Bayern Munich. The England captain finished ahead of Kylian Mbappé and Lamine Yamal in the voting, while also collecting the Gerd Müller Trophy for his goalscoring exploits.

Despite the significance of Kane’s achievement, Danks believes that the striker’s professionalism and relentless determination will ensure that his attention quickly returns to football.

“I know Harry very well,” Danks told reporters. “His focus will now be on scoring his next goal and his performances in the upcoming matches.”

The Bayern assistant also emphasized that Kane’s individual recognition reflected positively on the entire club, which celebrated its first Ballon d’Or winner since Karl-Heinz Rummenigge in 1981.

Danks is currently deputizing for Bayern head coach Vincent Kompany, who remains sidelined through illness. The Englishman confirmed that Kane and Bayern’s other representatives at Monday’s ceremony were expected to return from London on Tuesday and participate in afternoon training.

Kane is expected to be available for Wednesday evening’s DFB-Pokal fixture against Magdeburg, when the newly crowned Ballon d’Or winner will have his first opportunity to add to his goalscoring tally since receiving the award.

#3244. Warriors’ Yaxel Lendeborg makes stunning ‘wet the bed’ admission after crushing Nuggets

📅 Oct 27, 2026  |  Category: Sports  |  Read Source →

DENVER — It's been easy to forget at times in his first four NBA games that it wasn't too long ago Yaxel Lendeborg was just another guy on a basketball team at a junior college in the middle of the desert, five hours from anywhere. Like, not even four years ago.

One person it hasn't been lost on, despite his outspokenness, is Lendeborg.

The Warriors rookie's willingness to say whatever is on his mind, on the court or in press conferences, has already earned him a certain reputation. But it has also resulted in an endless stream of soundbites, some of which offer some actual insight.

For example, Lendeborg didn't hold back on Peacock after the Warriors' 134-114 win over the Nuggets on Monday night. He led the way with 31 points, 12 rebounds and six assists, the best performance in a historic start to his career — but not even he saw this coming.

Yaxel Lendeborg (1) of the Golden State Warriors celebrates during a game against the Denver Nuggets at Ball Arena in Denver on Monday, Oct. 26, 2026. NBAE via Getty Images

"I definitely thought I was gonna wet the bed," Lendeborg said. "Definitely didn't have the best hopes for what I was gonna do."

While that's the open book we've come to know, it doesn't exactly express the kind of confidence that would lead a 24-year-old rookie to chirp back-and-forth with Luka Doncic.

It sounds like the rookie who took six tepid shots in his first preseason game — a little more than three weeks ago, that's where Lendeborg was. Inside a 10,000-capacity gym in Honolulu, he made one shot and finished with four points in an exhibition loss to the Clippers.

What happened afterward might have changed the course of Warriors history.

"After the game in Hawaii, where I think I had like three points and only took like three shots," Lendeborg said, undercounting on both, he approached Steph Curry and Draymond Green.

"I asked them, you know, just for some guidance and advice. They told me, just try to be confident, be aggressive and play through the mistakes. I'm gonna be in a position to do that. Just hearing that from those two guys, I was coming into the game timid, like I don't want to mess up what they've got going on. And they pretty much just gave me free roam.

"So they helped me out, and from then on, I just vowed to be a little more aggressive."

Curry, Green and coach Steve Kerr all backed up the message publicly after Lendeborg's underwhelming preseason debut: They didn't just want him to be aggressive — they needed him to be. If nothing else, Lendeborg has proven to be a quick learner.

Lendeborg has scored in double digits in every game since — eight including the remaining four in the preseason — averaging 20.1 per contest on 13.1 shots. He's the first Warriors rookie to reach double figures in the first four games of his career since Chris Webber in 1993.

"He's definitely getting better each and every day," Green said. "It's extremely exciting because there's so much he don't know. He don't know a lot."

Golden State Warriors forward Yaxel Lendeborg hangs from the rim after dunking the ball for a basket in the second half of an NBA basketball game against the Denver Nuggets, Monday, Oct. 26, 2026, in Denver. AP Photo/David Zalubowski

Lendeborg said the game is already slowing down for him, at least offensively. He acknowledged he still has lots to learn on the other end of the court.

"I still have to figure out my weakside help," Lendeborg said. "I gave up a lot of buckets down low. Just thinking my teammates gonna get back, and they're thinking I'm gonna stay there. So it's just a little confusing."

Green has been more than eager to teach Lendeborg all of the tips, tricks and hints he's learned over his 12 years in the NBA. Advice has been coming at him like a firehose.

And Lendeborg just keeps gulping it all down.

"I find myself talking to him the entire game," Green said, "just telling him something different. And I think what's most of it is he can apply it right away. Like you tell him something, and he comes down next play, and it happens. He applies it right away. So that's special in itself."

When it's not coming from Green, it's coming from Kerr.

"It's definitely gonna be trial and error for me, but I really appreciate him for continuing to talk to me, have patience with me," Lendeborg said. "He's not screaming at me; he's talking to me like a human. … There's nothing for me, to be like, acting arrogant, or in a negative way toward it."

Lendeborg, it seems, doesn't miss a moment to learn something new. Even in his postgame interviews. Back on the Peacock set with Tracy McGrady, Vince Carter and Carmelo Anthony, Lendeborg turned the questions around on the trio of NBA legends.

"Do you guys have any advice, just to keep going, things I gotta work on?" Lendeborg asked the hosts. "Things that you guys see that I don't see?"

It's a Master's level course on the league for a player who just four seasons ago was averaging single digits for Arizona Western College, an NJCAA school on the western border in Yuma.

Golden State Warriors' Yaxel Lendeborg (1) drives against Denver Nuggets' DeMar DeRozan (10) during the fourth quarter of an NBA basketball game at Ball Arena in Denver, Monday, Oct. 26, 2026. Getty Images

Since then, of course, Lendeborg has gone on to win a national title at Michigan and earned his No. 11 overall selection in this past June's draft. That's a steep climb, and it would appear that Lendeborg's arrow is still pointing sharply up and to the right.

"I think that's the exciting part – where this can go," Kerr said after Lendeborg's 31-point night. "Because there's still probably 10 plays that I can pull up on tape, either mistakes defensively or offensively not recognizing something that he needs to recognize. Despite all that, he just dominated the game and took over. At 24, in his fourth game. So, pretty impressive."

#3901. Best factory layout in Moon Sweepers

📅 Oct 27, 2026  |  Category: Other  |  Read Source →

The ultimate aim in Moon Sweepers is to create your own automated factory that makes you profit without your active participation. In simple terms, I am talking about automation and making full use of it. Let’s say the total space on the moon is limited, and an efficient design is going to make things easier.

For this guide, you need to have access to all the endgame features. Until then, it’s best to do everything manually. For this setup, we will be using Capsule Catchers, Pressure Chambers, and Stone Presses. However, the aim is to maximize the efficiency of Conveyor Belts since you can connect at most 12 of them.

For our optimal setup, we will use two Capsule Catchers, four Pressure Chambers, and eight Stone Presses. However, let’s first understand how the method works by breaking down the size.

First, place the Capsule Catcher. For maximum efficiency, you want to use four bots for a singe catcher. Next, there will be two sets of Conveyor Belts from the Capsule Catcher.

Place two Pressure Chambers. As I said, there are two sets of Conveyor Belts from the Capsule Catcher: One for Dust, and one for Sand.

Put a Splitter at one of the two Conveyor Belts connected to the Capsule Catcher. Draw two more belts from the Splitter and connect each to the Pressure Chambers. Now, the Capsule Catcher will send Dust to the Pressure Chambers and produce Moon Sand. We have one of our two key ingredients.

Next, place four Stone Presses. We will connect two Stone Presses with one Pressure Chamber. To do so, put a Splitter at the end of the Conveyor Belt that connects the two.

If I name my Pressure Chambers as PC1 and PC2 (suppose), Stone Press 1 and Stone Press 2 will be connected to PC1, and Stone Press 3 and 4 will be connected to PC2. Make sure that they’re connected to the correct paths (Sand must go to the terminals that accept Sand).

Now, put Splitters at the end of the Conveyor Belts that will deliver the Dust from the Capsule Catchers. Put the first Splitter to break the path into two paths, and one more on each to further split the two paths into four.

Each of these four belt paths will be connected to the Dust terminal of your Stone Presses. Now, your Stone Presses have Sand and Dust, and they can produce Moon Stone. This has the best rate of returns in terms of profits.

Finally, use Mergers to combine the output of all four Presses into a single Conveyor Belt that will be connected to your base.

For maximum efficiency, you’ll want to do this setup with two Capsule Catchers, four Pressure Chambers and eight Stone Presses. The method remains the same, but the design is just going to be more complex.

#417. Illinois man arrested after allegedly sending suspicious powder to Congress members using his return address

📅 Oct 27, 2026  |  Category: Other  |  Read Source →

Matthew Unglesbee admitted to sending the letters to four unidentified politicians, officials say

An Illinois man accused of sending letters containing suspicious white powder to four members of Congress has been arrested after he allegedly wrote his own name and return address on the envelopes, according to authorities.

Matthew Unglesbee, 36, of Quincy, remained in the custody of US marshals on Tuesday on federal charges of perpetrating a hoax and sending threatening communications through the mail.

In an eight-page criminal complaint unsealed on Monday, Charis Hobbs – a taskforce agent with the FBI’s Springfield office – said Unglesbee admitted during an interview on Sunday that he sent the letters to the four unidentified politicians.

Unglesbee told the agent that the substance he placed inside the envelopes was salt – because it “kind of looks like” anthrax, the complaint said. Preliminary testing did not reveal the presence of any biological or chemical toxin.

“Each letter contained a white granular substance and a handwritten letter which read ‘ha ha’,” the court filing said. A letter to an unnamed US senator also contained granules and read “Hah enjoy the gift”.

On each of the four white, letter-sized envelopes, the agent said, Unglesbee wrote his name and home address in Quincy.

None of the staffers who handled the letters at any of the four Congress members’ offices in northern Illinois opened the envelopes, the complaint said. The offices had alerted each other to look out for mailings with Unglesbee’s name on them.

FBI personnel “trained in handling weapons of mass destruction, and threats of biochemical toxins” were sent to three of the politicians’ offices on Wednesday after staff called authorities, and hazardous materials (hazmat) response teams attended two of them.

Having retrieved the communications with Unglesbee’s address on them, FBI agents visited his home, where they discovered another envelope – empty and addressed, but not sent, to one of the same Congress member’s Washington DC office.

The suspect told investigators that he mailed four letters “after hearing someone in a restaurant mention the news story of other letters being sent”, the filing said. Earlier in October, the FBI and US Postal Inspection Service said they were investigating multiple reports of suspicious letters sent to state officials and election offices across the country.

Unglesbee denied participating in the other suspicious mailings. But he allegedly told investigators that he anticipated the Congress members would “take it serious” because the powder he sent could be perceived to be anthrax, which he said he knew was dangerous and would cause hazmat teams to have to respond.

“Unglesbee claimed he was joking but recognized the recipients would not know he was joking and could perceive it as a threat,” the complaint said.

In a justice department press release on Monday announcing Unglesbee’s arrest, Ryan Presley, special agent in charge of the FBI’s Springfield field office, said authorities were focused on “keeping our public institutions safe from intimidation”.

He said: “Mailing unknown substances to congressional offices creates fear and disrupts the public institutions vital to our democracy. We, along with our partners, will remain vigilant in identifying and holding accountable those whose actions threaten to intimidate public officials or disrupt our democratic systems.”

Unglesbee was tentatively scheduled to appear for a detention hearing on Wednesday afternoon in federal court in Springfield.

Apple has rolled out its latest iOS 27.2 update for iPhones and iPads, featuring expanded language support and improved memory functions for Siri AI, suggestions in messages, and Apple Pencil support for the foldable iPhone Duo.

With the new software update, Siri AI can now be used in French, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese, and Spanish, in beta. Apple says the AI assistant can now remember details from past conversations and answer accordingly. The ability for AI assistants to remember a person’s preferences have been a key area of focus for AI model makers, including OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google, whose assistants now do a better job at recalling facts and choices related to users.

Apart from its own suggestions and answers, Siri can also tap into sources like photos attached in Messages, calendar event attendees, voicemails, Safari history, and reading lists. Users can define what apps they might want to exclude from Siri AI’s memories.

While the iPhone Duo is facing some display-related problems for early buyers, Apple is adding more features with the update. iOS 27.2 brings Apple Pencil support for the foldable in apps like Notes and Preview. What’s more, users can use the Edge Light effect to highlight their face better during video calls.

The new software version also adds features for FaceTime, including streaming via front and rear camera simultaneously through Dual Capture for group call, and the ability to add a third-party sign language interpreter or live captioner to a call.

Users on iOS 27.2 will get suggestions to add a message as an item in a reminder or an event in a calendar. Plus, users can edit captions or add comments in Shared Albums along with reminders of the expiry date of the folder.

The update also refreshes the Podcast app, which will show new episodes from the shows you follow and also the episodes that you are currently listening to.

Apple has also added Multiview to iPhone so viewers can watch up to four live sport streams simultaneously on Apple TV such as Major League Soccer (MLS), Formula 1 (F1) in the U.S., and Friday Night Baseball doubleheaders during the Major League Baseball (MLB) season. Users can also view multiple feeds of F1 during the broadcast.

iOS 27.2 also packs in over 160 security fixes, according to the company’s release documentation. Many of the bug fixes were also backported so that users running the older iOS 26 software can benefit from the fixes.

Apple says that about 80% of iPhone owners are still running iOS 26.

Meanwhile, Apple backported a specific bug fix to users of iOS 18, first released in 2024, which Apple says may have been exploited “in an extremely sophisticated attack against specific targeted individuals on versions of iOS before iOS 27.” This is standard language that Apple uses when someone’s phone has been compromised by government spyware or a law enforcement forensic device, but did not provide specifics about who was exploiting it.
