That’s about as big as Tesla’s entire Houston Robotaxi geofence after this week’s expansion, and Waymo is adding it in cities where it already runs with nobody in the driver’s seat.

The company announced the expansion in a short post on its blog today. Here’s what changes in each market, with Waymo’s updated service area maps:

Service now runs down the coast into the South Bay, through Manhattan Beach and Redondo Beach to Torrance.

The zone pushes further south, and riders can now get to the zoo.

Coverage on the east side nearly doubles, and there’s more service downtown.

The service area now stretches north to La Jolla, taking in University City and Clairemont along the way.

Waymo is pushing further into the South Bay, including a connection to BART at Berryessa station in San Jose. The service area now runs from San Francisco down the Peninsula to San Jose and Campbell.

Waymo didn’t break out square mileage by city, and it didn’t say how many vehicles it’s adding to cover the new ground. The updated maps are already in the Waymo app.

Some of these markets are very young. Waymo opened Nashville to public riders in April with a zone of about 60 square miles covering downtown, Midtown, Music Row, and East Nashville. San Diego only went public on September 1, with roughly 50 square miles between the beach and I-15 that barely crossed north of I-8. So getting to La Jolla less than two months later is a big jump.

In May, Waymo pushed its coverage past 1,400 square miles across 11 cities, which is bigger than the state of Rhode Island. Since then, it went driverless in Las Vegas, launched San Diego, and now says it operates in 15 US metros with more than 500,000 paid rides per week. It’s even mapping Toronto for a 2028 launch.

Tesla is also drawing bigger maps. After launching Robotaxi in Dallas and Houston with tiny geofences in April, it stretched Houston to about 254 square miles this week, according to Robotaxi Tracker. But the tracker counts 34 vehicles in Houston.

And on the metric that reflects actual use, it’s not close. Waymo logged 3.9 million rider-only miles per week in Q2 2026, while Tesla’s driverless fleet did 558,000 miles in its latest reported week, mostly in Austin. That’s about 7x, and it comes on the same day Elon Musk claimed the robotaxi race against Waymo “was never a real contest.”

#3371. Rockstar workers in the UK will hold a union vote just as GTA 6 is released

📅 Oct 27, 2026  |  Category: Technology  |  Read Source →

A legal case alleging union-busting at the game developer will also wrap next month.

Most gamers may be focused on the countdown to the release of Grand Theft Auto 6 on November 19, but there will also be a fair bit of drama unfolding behind the scenes at developer Rockstar Games over the coming weeks. According to a social media post from Bloomberg's Jason Schreier, workers at Rockstar's UK offices are reportedly scheduled to vote on recognizing their branch of the Independent Workers Union of Great Britain (IWUGB) from November 12 to November 26. If the employee vote is a success, the Rockstar Game Workers Union would become the largest recognized union in the UK's game industry.

Adding another layer to the combined cultural and corporate hype around late November, an employment tribunal between Rockstar and the IWUGB that was scheduled to end earlier in October has also been extended. That case is now expected to have closing arguments delivered on November 17 or 18, just before the GTA 6 launch. The IWUGB brought a complaint against Rockstar after the game developer laid off more than 30 employees in October 2025, most of whom were either union members or engaged in organizing efforts. The move raised alarms that the studio was engaged in union-busting, although the company claimed the employees were let go due to misconduct related to sharing confidential information. The Rockstar Game Workers Union, covering people at all of the company's offices across the UK, was announced in May 2026.

In a feature piece earlier this month that interviewed several people from Rockstar, Schreier reported that the unionizing workers who remained have been in ongoing discussion with the company's management in the intervening months. "We go where the team goes," Jennifer Kolbe, head of publishing at Rockstar, told Schreier. "So if they want a union, they will have a union."

#4769. Ripple, Robinhood expand partnership to bring RLUSD to retail and institutional markets

📅 Oct 27, 2026  |  Category: Technology  |  Read Source →

Ripple also expanded its collaboration with BNY on digital asset custody and partnered with C2FO to bring trade finance settlement onto the XRP Ledger.

Ripple and Robinhood have expanded their partnership to bring the RLUSD stablecoin to Robinhood’s retail and institutional businesses, alongside new integrations spanning treasury management, lending and trading services.

RLUSD is already available on Robinhood Chain and Bitstamp by Robinhood, with a launch in the Robinhood app planned for later this year, according to a Tuesday announcement coinciding with Ripple’s Swell 2026 conference in New York. Robinhood also plans to use the stablecoin on the XRP Ledger for institutional settlement.

Robinhood will also adopt Ripple Treasury to manage its global cash and liquidity operations, including payment processing, cash flow forecasting and intercompany transactions. The integration extends the partnership beyond customer-facing services to Robinhood’s internal financial infrastructure.

The partnership also expands Robinhood’s existing relationship with Ripple Prime, which provides financing services to Bitstamp by Robinhood and plans to introduce perpetual futures trading on the exchange later this year.

Launched in December 2024, RLUSD has reached a market capitalization of $2.57 billion, with nearly $1 billion issued on the XRP Ledger, according to DeFiLlama data. Robinhood reported 28.6 million funded customers globally as of August, offering a potentially significant distribution channel for the stablecoin.

Related: Majority of Americans want candidates who support US-based financial technology: Poll

Ripple also announced on Tuesday an expanded partnership with financial services giant BNY, which now supports custody of XRP and RLUSD through its Digital Asset Custody platform. The companies plan to work together to allow BNY clients to mint and redeem RLUSD directly through the bank and broaden support for tokenized assets issued on the XRP Ledger.

Separately, Ripple partnered with working-capital platform C2FO to bring financing and settlement for its new Instant Pay service onto the XRP Ledger, using RLUSD. The service is designed to let suppliers receive payments against approved invoices in seconds rather than waiting days for traditional bank settlement.

A C2FO spokesperson told Cointelegraph the service is currently in testing, with a controlled launch and the first RLUSD transactions on the XRP Ledger expected by the end of the year.

The string of announcements on Tuesday followed Ripple’s receipt of final approval from the US Office of the Comptroller of the Currency on Monday to establish a national trust bank. The institution will manage reserves backing RLUSD and provide digital asset custody services, while stablecoin issuance will remain with Ripple’s New York-regulated subsidiary.
